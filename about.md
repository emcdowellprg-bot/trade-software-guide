# About these guides

These guides are independent. Vendors do not pay for placement, and a vendor cannot buy a higher rank. The site exists to answer a specific buying question for a small service business, using the vendors' own published pages.

## How a tool gets evaluated

Each guide is written for one trade and one situation, such as a 1–3 truck HVAC company choosing between two field-service products. The comparison sticks to the parts of the job that change the week: scheduling and dispatch, quotes, invoicing, payments, maintenance agreements, accounting sync, the mobile app, and the price of the plan that actually includes those features.

A claim stays in the guide only when a vendor's pricing page, product page, or help center supports it. The price is marked with the date it was checked and a link to the source. If a page is too vague to support the claim, or two official pages disagree, the guide either leaves the claim out or labels it unverified. Marketing lines about average revenue growth are the vendor's numbers, not a result measured for this site.

These pages are not hands-on reviews unless a guide says the product was used. The first guide was not.

## Affiliate disclosure

Some links may become affiliate links. This never changes our rankings.

There are no affiliate or referral links on the site today. If that changes, a commission will not move a product up or down the page, and it will not decide which plan we describe as the fit for a given shop. Rankings, where a guide makes one, follow the published fit for the business in the title.
