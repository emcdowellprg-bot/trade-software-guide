A cleaning owner hiring a first cleaner is buying software for a week that used to fit on one phone: the Tuesday house, the every-other-week house, and now a second person who has to see that list, mark the rooms, clock the visit, and get paid either as an employee or as a 1099. This page compares [ZenMaid](https://get.zenmaid.com/pricing), [BookingKoala](https://www.bookingkoala.com/pricing/), [Jobber](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/), [Housecall Pro](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/), and [FreshBooks](https://www.freshbooks.com/pricing) on that step, from one login to two. It is a comparison of the vendors' pricing pages and help centers, checked on November 26, 2026. We have not run any of these products on a house.

The one-person choice, including Square, is the [solo house cleaning guide]({{ '/guides/best-software-solo-house-cleaning-business/' | relative_url }}). Recurring houses, cleaner texts, and the payroll math, with ZenMaid next to Jobber, are the [ZenMaid vs Jobber guide]({{ '/guides/zenmaid-vs-jobber-recurring-maid-service/' | relative_url }}). The booking form and the charge after the clean are the [ZenMaid vs BookingKoala guide]({{ '/guides/zenmaid-vs-bookingkoala-online-booking-payments/' | relative_url }}). Routes, review requests, and a crew of up to about ten are the [Jobber vs Housecall Pro guide]({{ '/guides/jobber-vs-housecall-pro-residential-cleaning/' | relative_url }}). This page stays with the first extra person.

This page does not decide whether that person is an employee or a contractor. It says which label each vendor uses, and which pay tool the vendor documents for that label.

## Who it's for

Use this if you run residential house cleaning or a maid service, you are still the person who builds the week, and you are about to give one cleaner a login. That cleaner might be a W-2 employee or a 1099. The jobs that change are assignment, the phone app, the checklist, a clock-in, and the pay calculation.

It is not a commercial janitorial system. It is not a crew of several people on named routes. Housecall Pro's HVAC, plumbing, and electrical packages are out of scope. Cleaning is on Housecall Pro's standard plans. The pricing page fetched for this guide is headed for an "Other" business, and the pricing FAQ names cleaning alongside the trades.

## Quick answer

Prices below are the ones to budget. The pricing section has the date, the billing term, and the page each number came from. Jobber's card also shows a new-customer promotion for subscriptions started before November 30, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. PT. Housecall Pro prints 50% off for three months against the month-to-month price. **A checklist, a clock-in point, and a pay number you can take to the bank or to a payroll service.** [ZenMaid Pro](https://get.zenmaid.com/pricing) is **$39 per month**. The owner profile is included. The [cost article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/2423512-how-much-does-zenmaid-cost), dated June 4, 2026, prices each added cleaner, contractor, or office manager at **$14 per month** on Pro. An owner plus one cleaner is $39 + $14 = **$53** if that rate still applies. That total is arithmetic from the cost article. The pricing page fetched on November 26, 2026 prints $39 and a team-size control, and it does not print $14. Pro lists digital checklists, the mobile app with cleaner GPS, and payroll. The payroll article calculates hourly pay, a percentage of the original visit price, or a flat rate, then exports or emails the summary. It does not describe a direct deposit or a tax filing. Starter is **$19**, plus **$4** for a cleaner profile in that same article, and Starter's card does not list checklists, GPS, or payroll.

**The second person is another provider, and five providers already fit the plan.** [BookingKoala Starter](https://www.bookingkoala.com/pricing/) is **$27 per month**, with 5 providers or 5 GB. A provider performs the service. The page's example is a maid, and each person on a team counts. Two people who clean stay on $27. Checklists, scheduling individuals or teams, and a provider dashboard are checked on Starter. The native app and GPS clock-in are checked on Growing, at **$57 per month**, which includes 15 providers. The provider-payments article can calculate a fixed amount, a percentage, or an hourly amount, and it can send that amount through Stripe or PayPal or mark it cash or check. That article does not name a plan, and it does not describe a payroll-tax filing.

**An hourly employee, a checklist, and hours that sync to Gusto.** [Jobber Connect](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/) for one user is **$99 per month billed annually**, **$119 per month on a 1-year term billed monthly**, or **$139 month-to-month**. The pricing page says an additional user is **$29 per month**. One user plus one extra user is $128, $148, or $168 if that $29 applies on each billing term. Those are calculations, not a two-user pack. The Connect card lists checklists and time tracking. The [Connect article](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-connect-plan/) says the plan supports up to five users. [Core](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-core-plan/) supports one user, and the Core card does not list checklists or time tracking. Jobber's confirm-payroll article says Jobber does not pay employees. Approved hours can sync to Gusto on Connect or higher, in the United States, and only for hourly employees in Gusto. A contractor pay type does not sync.

**Live phone GPS, checklist automations, and a plan that already includes five logins.** Housecall Pro Basic is the one-user card: **$59 per month billed annually**, or **$79 month-to-month**. The pricing page does not print a dollar amount for a second user on Basic. Essentials includes five users at **$149 billed annually**, or **$189 month-to-month**. That card lists checklist automations and employee GPS from the phone, with no hardware. The timesheet article describes a mobile clock-in and a location pin, and it does not name Basic, Essentials, or MAX. Housecall Pro Payroll is a separate product. The payroll feature page says it supports 1099 contractors and does not print a price. A resources article updated October 7, 2025 states $40 per month plus $6 per employee. The pricing page fetched November 26, 2026 does not print that figure. See unverified claims.

**The invoice, a login, and payroll taxes, with the houses kept on another calendar.** FreshBooks Plus is **$43 per month**. A team member is **$11 per month**. An unassigned profile is free, and that profile cannot collaborate until you invite it. The first cleaner who tracks time is the $11 invite, so Plus plus one invited cleaner is **$54** before payroll. FreshBooks Payroll, powered by Gusto, is **$40 per month plus $6 per month per user** on the pricing cards. The [payroll page](https://www.freshbooks.com/payroll-software) says **$40 per month plus $6 per month for each employee**, with unlimited payroll runs, direct deposit, and automatic federal and state tax filing. The pages checked do not describe a calendar of houses or a dispatch board.

Buy ZenMaid Pro if the cleaner needs the cleaning app, a checklist, a clock-in point, and a percentage, flat, or hourly pay figure, and you will pay them outside the software. Buy BookingKoala Starter if two providers fit the $27 plan and a browser login is enough. Buy Growing if that cleaner needs the native app or GPS clock-in. Buy Jobber Connect, one user plus one additional user, if the cleaner is an hourly employee and the hours should land in Gusto. Buy Housecall Pro Essentials if you want the published plan that includes a second login, checklist automations, and live phone GPS. Buy FreshBooks Plus plus one team member if the schedule stays in one of the products above and the new bill is the invoice, the login, and optional tax-filing payroll.

## ZenMaid

Each card says SMS charges are not included. The [cost article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/2423512-how-much-does-zenmaid-cost), dated June 4, 2026, says only the owner profile is included. A cleaner, a contractor, or an office manager is an added profile, and only an active role is charged. The first hire is that added profile. The same article says annual plans exist on request and does not print an annual price.

**Scheduling.** Starter includes scheduling with calendar, dispatch, and map views, and it stops at 40 appointments in the month. The pricing FAQ says that at the cap you cannot add another appointment that month, and the account sends you to upgrade. Pro lists unlimited appointments. The [cleaner app article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/6968490-zenmaid-mobile-app-overview-for-cleaners), dated September 28, 2026, shows the assigned day from earliest to latest, and a map that numbers the stops in schedule order. Customers cannot sign in. The recurring calendar, and which texts are on which plan, are the [ZenMaid vs Jobber guide]({{ '/guides/zenmaid-vs-jobber-recurring-maid-service/' | relative_url }}).

**The phone, the checklist, and the clock.** The app is on Starter without cleaner GPS. Pro lists the app with cleaner GPS, digital checklists, and payroll. The [clock article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/1768986-how-cleaners-clock-in-and-out-using-the-zenmaid-mobile-app), dated August 26, 2026, says a cleaner clocks in and out of an appointment once. An app clock-in stores a timestamp that cannot be edited. A clock-in entered on the website can be edited. The app can store a GPS point if the device permits it. The app article is more specific: the location is recorded when Clock In or Clock Out is pressed, tracking is not continuous, and it does not run outside the app. The clock article does not name Starter, Pro, or Pro Max. Starter's card is the line that says the app has no cleaner GPS.

The app article also describes the checklist on the phone. Sections are rooms. Marking an item stores the time. If another cleaner marks an item, the app shows that cleaner's name and the time. The Pro card is the plan gate this guide uses for digital checklists. The article itself does not name a plan.

**Payroll.** The Pro card lists payroll. The [payroll article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/1767506-how-to-run-payroll-in-zenmaid), dated March 17, 2026, does not name a plan. A cleaner can be hourly, revenue share, a fixed flat rate, or a variable flat rate. For hourly pay, clocked time is used when the cleaner clocks in and out. Otherwise the scheduled appointment time is used. Revenue share is calculated on the original appointment price, before discounts, taxes, overcharges, and tips. Tips are listed and are not added until someone enters them as an extra. One payroll run cannot mix hourly and revenue share for the same cleaner. After approval, the summary can be printed, exported as a CSV, or emailed. Cleaners can be allowed to open their own approved payroll on the web app. The article describes that summary. It does not describe a direct deposit or a payroll-tax filing. A percentage of the house, or a flat amount per house, is a number you take somewhere else to pay the person.

The cost article charges the same add-on for a cleaner employee and a cleaner contractor. The label does not change the ZenMaid price.

The trial is 14 days. The pricing FAQ says you can cancel the trial or the subscription from the account settings, and that the plan is month-to-month with no contract. The page does not say whether the trial asks for a card.

## What to watch out for

Count the login the plan actually sells. A ZenMaid owner is included, and the cleaner is a profile. A Jobber user is anyone who opens the schedule, in the office or in the field. A BookingKoala provider is someone who performs the service, and a team member counts. A Housecall Pro Basic seat is one user. A FreshBooks unassigned profile is free and cannot do the work. The invited role is the $11 seat. A helper who rides along and never logs in is not one of those charges. A helper who needs the day's list on a phone is.

The first hire is often the moment the plan changes, not the moment a small add-on appears. ZenMaid Starter can hold a cleaner profile, and it still omits checklists, GPS, and payroll, and it still stops at 40 appointments. Jobber Core can describe assigning a team in its workflow article, and the same article caps the plan at one user. Housecall Pro Basic can dispatch, and the card includes one user. BookingKoala is the exception in this set: the $27 plan already includes five providers, so the second cleaner does not move the tier by headcount alone.

Payroll is a different object in each product. ZenMaid Pro's article calculates the cleaner's number and exports it. BookingKoala's article can calculate a fixed amount, a percentage, or an hourly amount, and it can send that amount through Stripe or PayPal. Neither article describes a tax filing. Jobber's article says Jobber does not pay the employee. Gusto, on Connect or higher, receives hours for hourly US employees and does not receive a contractor. Housecall Pro's Gusto article is the same kind of hourly sync, and it does not name the scheduling plan. Housecall Pro's own payroll product says it can pay employees and 1099 contractors. Its current price is not on the pricing page. FreshBooks Payroll is the add-on that says it files payroll taxes and pays by direct deposit, on top of the $11 seat.

A clock-in is not the same thing as a live map. ZenMaid's app article records a point when the cleaner taps Clock In or Clock Out, on the Pro card's GPS line, and it says the track is not live. BookingKoala's GPS clock-in is Growing, and it can follow a path or store timestamps only. Jobber's app timer stores a waypoint at the start and the stop, on the plans that include time tracking. The automatic geofence timer is Grow. The live vehicle feed named on Connect is FleetSharp, and the article does not say the hardware is included. Housecall Pro's Essentials card is the live phone map during business hours. The timesheet pin is described in a different article, and that article does not name the plan.

Match the pay type to the product before you pick the product for the tax form. A percentage of the visit is in the ZenMaid payroll article and in the BookingKoala provider-pay article. It is not what Jobber syncs to Gusto, and it is not what Housecall Pro syncs to Gusto. Those two syncs are hourly employees. A 1099 cleaner is a supported label on Housecall Pro's payroll FAQ and on FreshBooks' September 18, 2026 payroll article. ZenMaid charges the same profile price for a cleaner employee and a cleaner contractor. BookingKoala's reimbursement setting uses the 1099 and W-2 labels and does not describe filing either form.

Jobber's $99 Connect price is a one-year term. The no-commitment price is $139, and the second user is a further $29 on the card. Housecall Pro's $149 Essentials price is the annual figure. The month-to-month figure is $189, and the FAQ says there is no long-term contract. ZenMaid's $39 and BookingKoala's $27 are the month-to-month prices those pages print. FreshBooks' $43 is the regular monthly Plus price. The 90% and 50% offers, and Jobber's lower introductory column, are promotions. Budget the regular price.

The cleaning and home-service pages lead with results: hours saved, revenue growth, and counts of pros. Those are the vendors' aggregates. Nothing on this page comes from our using any of these apps on a house.

**Which app shows my cleaner today's houses?**

ZenMaid's cleaner app lists the assigned day. The app is on Starter, and cleaner GPS is on the Pro card. BookingKoala's provider dashboard is checked on Starter, in the browser. The native app is Growing. Jobber's app is how a Connect user sees the visit and starts a timer. Housecall Pro's iOS and Android app is on every plan, and Basic is still one user, so the second person is an Essentials seat unless a Basic extra-user price is offered at signup. FreshBooks' phone app is the accounting app. The pages checked do not describe a list of houses for the day.

**Which plan has a cleaning checklist?**

ZenMaid lists digital checklists on Pro. BookingKoala checks checklists on Starter. Premium adds realtime progress, dependencies, and export. Jobber lists checklists on the Connect card. The checklist article says select plans and does not print the word Connect. Housecall Pro lists checklist automations on Essentials. The checklist overview does not name a plan. FreshBooks' pages checked do not describe a cleaning checklist.

**Can I see where the cleaner was when they clocked in?**

ZenMaid's app article stores a GPS point at clock-in and clock-out, and it says there is no live track. That GPS line is on the Pro card. BookingKoala's GPS clock-in is on Growing, and it can store a path or timestamps only. Jobber stores a GPS waypoint when an app timer starts and stops. Location timers that run from the phone's position are on Grow. Housecall Pro's timesheet article can show a clock-in pin and a clock-out pin, and it does not name a plan. The live map during business hours is the employee GPS line on Essentials.

**Which tool calculates a percentage, and which one files payroll taxes?**

ZenMaid's payroll article calculates a percentage of the original visit price, a flat amount, or hourly pay from the clock or the schedule, and then exports the summary. FreshBooks Payroll says it files federal and state payroll taxes. The payroll page prices it at $40 a month plus $6 per employee, on top of the plan.

**I am hiring a 1099, not a W-2. What does the software say?**

ZenMaid's cost article charges the same added-profile price for a cleaner contractor and a cleaner employee. BookingKoala's pay article does not file a 1099. Its reimbursement setting uses the label "1099 in the US" for contractors. Jobber's Gusto article does not sync contractor, salary, or commission types. Housecall Pro's payroll FAQ says 1099 contractors are supported, and its Gusto article syncs employees who are paid by the hour. FreshBooks has a contractor role at the same $11 seat, and the September 18, 2026 payroll article says the payroll add-on can pay 1099 contractors.
