A solo house cleaner is usually buying software for a week of houses that already repeat: the weekly or every-other-week clean, a first-clean or deep-clean quote, the invoice for that visit or for the month, and a card or bank payment that does not depend on chasing a check. This page compares [Square](https://squareup.com/us/en/appointments/pricing), [ZenMaid](https://get.zenmaid.com/pricing), [BookingKoala](https://www.bookingkoala.com/pricing/), [Jobber](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/), [Housecall Pro](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/), and [FreshBooks](https://www.freshbooks.com/pricing) on that work. It is a comparison of the vendors' pricing pages and help centers, checked on November 22, 2026. We have not run any of these products on a job.

## Who it's for

Use this if you are the owner-operator of a residential house cleaning or maid service. You book the houses, you clean them, and you collect. You do not have employees yet. A later cleaner who needs a login is a different price, and that price is in the pricing section.

This page stays with one person. A crew of up to about ten, and the choice between Jobber and Housecall Pro for that crew, is the [Jobber vs Housecall Pro guide for residential cleaning]({{ '/guides/jobber-vs-housecall-pro-residential-cleaning/' | relative_url }}). A commercial janitorial account with a night supervisor, and Housecall Pro's HVAC, plumbing, or electrical package, are out of scope. Cleaning is on Housecall Pro's standard plans. The help center's industry-packages collection lists HVAC, electrical, and plumbing, and does not list cleaning. The pricing page fetched for this guide is headed for an "Other" business, and the pricing FAQ names cleaning alongside the trades.

## Quick answer

Prices below are the ones to budget. The pricing section has the date, the billing term, and the page each number came from.

**About 40 visits a month or fewer, in software built for a maid service.** [ZenMaid Starter](https://get.zenmaid.com/pricing) is **$19 per month**. The owner profile is included. Starter includes scheduling, invoicing, and online payments through a Stripe or Square account you connect. It stops at 40 appointments in that month. Booking forms, digital checklists, cleaner GPS, and the QuickBooks line are on Pro at **$39 per month**. The Pro card marks QuickBooks integration "COMING SOON." SMS is extra on every plan.

**A fuller route: one calendar, a quote, an invoice, and a card, with no visit cap on the entry plan.** [Jobber Core](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-core-plan/) is the one-person plan: **$29 per month billed annually**, or **$49 month-to-month**. Core includes the calendar, online booking, recurring jobs, quotes, invoices, and Jobber Payments. The pricing table lists cards at 2.9% + 30¢ and Tap to Pay at 2.7% + 30¢ on Core. Appointment reminders, automatic charging of a saved card, and QuickBooks Online are on Connect. One Connect user is **$99 per month billed annually**, or **$139 month-to-month**.

**No monthly subscription, a booking page, invoices, and card payments.** [Square Free](https://squareup.com/us/en/appointments/pricing) is **$0 per month** per location. You pay when a card or bank payment runs. The pricing page includes an online booking site, appointment reminders, unlimited staff calendars, estimates, and invoices, including recurring invoices. In-person cards are 2.6% + 15¢. Cards paid online or on an invoice are 3.3% + 30¢. A card keyed or kept on file is 3.5% + 15¢.

**The customer picks the clean on your site and sees the price.** [BookingKoala Starter](https://www.bookingkoala.com/pricing/) is **$27 per month**, with 5 providers or 5 GB. A provider is someone who performs the service, and the pricing page's example is a maid. Starter includes booking forms, a calendar, invoicing, and charging a card. BookingKoala does not add a processing fee of its own. The fee is Stripe, Square, PayPal, or Authorize.net. Text messages and the native apps are on Growing at **$57 per month**.

**Job costing and a price book on the entry plan.** Housecall Pro Basic is the one-user card: **$59 per month billed annually**, or **$79 month-to-month**, with a three-month promotion at $39.50 printed against the $79. Basic includes scheduling, estimates, invoices, a price book, job costing, and payments. Tap to Pay is 2.59%, plus $5 in any month that phone takes a Tap to Pay payment. QuickBooks Online requires Essentials or MAX. Recurring service plans are on MAX and hold up to 12 visits a year. The recurring-job article does not name a plan.

**The invoice and the books, and the customer pays a link.** FreshBooks Plus is the plan that fits a real customer list: **$43 per month**, or **$464.40 per year**. Plus allows 50 active and archived clients. Lite is $23 per month, or $248.40 per year, and it stops at 5 clients. A route of houses does not fit on Lite. FreshBooks documents estimates, invoices, deposits, and recurring invoices on Lite and up. The pages checked do not describe a job calendar. Keying a card, or charging a saved card, is the Advanced Payments add-on at $20 per month, and that charge is 3.5% + $0.30.

Buy ZenMaid Starter if the book is still small and you want the calendar in a cleaning product, at the lowest paid subscription that is built for that calendar. Buy Jobber Core if the route is already past 40 visits and one login needs quotes, recurring jobs, and a card on the phone. Buy Square Free if a booking page, an invoice, and Square's processing fee are enough and a monthly software bill is the thing you are trying to avoid. Buy BookingKoala Starter if the priced booking site is the product. Buy Housecall Pro Basic if job costing on the entry plan matters more than the lower subscription. Buy FreshBooks Plus if the invoicing app is replacing the books, and the schedule of houses is still kept somewhere else.

## Square

Square Free is the $0 plan on the [appointments pricing page](https://squareup.com/us/en/appointments/pricing). The subscription is $0 per location. The bill is the processing fee. Square Plus is $49 per month per location, and Square Premium is $149. A solo cleaner who is deciding whether any subscription is worth it is looking at Free.

**Scheduling.** Free includes an online booking site, booking integrations, appointment reminders, and unlimited staff calendars. The calendars tooltip says there is no employee limit. The [scheduling article](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/5349-schedule-and-accept-appointments) says an appointment can be a specific time, all day, or recurring, and that a repeat appointment starts at the first appointment's price until you change it. The article's audience includes Square Free subscribers with advanced bookings capabilities added. The numbered steps on the page fetched for this guide do not show the repetition control. See unverified claims. The pricing table marks Google Calendar sync and class booking on Plus and Premium, and not on Free. A cancellation policy can be displayed on Free. Charging a card for a no-show is Plus or Premium.

**Quotes.** Estimates are on Free. Multi-package estimates, more than one option in a single estimate, start on Plus.

**Invoicing.** Invoices and recurring invoices are on Free, on a daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly schedule. Deposits and automatic payment reminders are on Free. Custom invoice templates, batch invoices, and a milestone payment schedule start on Plus.

**Payments.** Rates are the [Square Free rates](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/5068-what-are-square-s-fees) on the pricing page and the fees article. Square says the rate is the same for every card brand.

- Tap, dip, or swipe: 2.6% + 15¢.
- Online, or a card paid on an invoice: 3.3% + 30¢.
- A card keyed in, or a card on file: 3.5% + 15¢.
- ACH on an invoice: 1%, with a $1 minimum. The Plus and Premium rows add a $10 cap. The Free row does not.
- Afterpay: 6% + 30¢.
- An international card adds 1.5%.
- Cash or check: no fee.

The pricing page says hardware is not required. Invoicing, payment links, a virtual terminal, and Tap to Pay on iPhone or Android are the no-hardware methods. Plus and Premium each have a 30-day trial, and the FAQ says you can cancel or switch those plans anytime. Free has no monthly subscription. You pay the processing fee when a payment runs.

## ZenMaid

ZenMaid's [pricing page](https://get.zenmaid.com/pricing) sells three month-to-month plans to a maid service. Starter is $19, Pro is $39, and Pro Max is $49. Each card says SMS charges are not included. The [cost article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/2423512-how-much-does-zenmaid-cost), dated June 4, 2026, says only the owner profile is included and every other profile is an extra charge. A solo cleaner who works from the owner profile is the $19 or $39 plan, not a per-cleaner add-on. The same article says annual plans exist on request and does not print an annual price.

**Scheduling.** Starter includes scheduling with calendar, dispatch, and map views, and it stops at 40 appointments in the month. The pricing FAQ says that at the cap you cannot add another appointment that month, and the account sends you to upgrade. The [recurring-service article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/1768662-how-to-add-a-daily-recurring-service-in-zenmaid) says each day is its own recurring appointment, set to weekly, and it does not name Starter, Pro, or Pro Max. Ten weekly houses are 40 visits in a four-week month and 50 in a five-week month. That is arithmetic from the cap, not a package ZenMaid sells. A route that crosses 40 visits in a month is Pro, which lists unlimited appointments.

The customer-facing form is a different gate. The Pro card lists booking forms with ZenMaid branding. Pro Max lists the same forms with your branding. Starter's card does not list them. The [booking article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/3417682-how-to-manage-your-bookings) says a customer who picked a recurring frequency is converted to a subscription, and a one-time request is converted to an appointment. The [time-slot article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/3518448-how-to-create-booking-form-time-slots-and-edit-bookable-days), dated July 3, 2026, says the form is not a live calendar, and that you tell the customer you will confirm the slot. Spotfinder, "easy scheduling of new recurring customers," is on the Pro card.

**Quotes.** The [quote-form article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/6278498-how-to-create-and-customize-an-estimate-or-quote-form-in-zenmaid) is a booking form renamed as an estimate, with prices shown or hidden. It does not name a plan. The pricing card is the plan gate for booking forms. The [pricing article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/3242732-how-to-price-your-booking-form) says bedroom, bathroom, square-footage, and hour sliders scale by a fixed step, extras are flat fees, and a recurrence option on the form can carry a discount.

**Invoicing and payments.** Starter includes invoicing and online payments with Stripe and Square. The [charge article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/12099721-ways-customers-can-be-charged-by-card-in-zenmaid), dated January 30, 2026, says a card has to be saved on the customer before ZenMaid can charge it, and that marking an appointment paid does not charge the card. You can charge one appointment, bulk-charge unpaid appointments, or charge from the contact. For a weekly or monthly clean, the same article says you charge each appointment, charge the unpaid invoices on one day, or put the month's total on one appointment in the series. Invoices are created by hand. When the customer pays the invoice, ZenMaid marks the linked appointment paid.

The [processor article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/4801218-connecting-a-credit-card-processor-to-zenmaid) says the account has to be Stripe or Square directly. Recurring or automatic charges are configured in Stripe or Square. ZenMaid does not start them. The same article says cards already stored in Stripe before the connection cannot be charged through ZenMaid. Square has an import for cards already saved there. ZenMaid does not publish the card rate. The rate is the processor's.

**QuickBooks and the phone.** The Pro card lists QuickBooks integration and prints "COMING SOON" on that line. The mobile app is on Starter without cleaner GPS. Pro adds GPS. The cost article says SMS bundles start at $14 for 125 messages. The pricing page says SMS is extra and does not print that bundle price. See unverified claims.

The trial is 14 days. The pricing FAQ says you can cancel the trial or the subscription from the account settings, and that the plan is month-to-month with no contract. The page does not say whether the trial asks for a card.

The page's examples include a maid, and it says each person on a team counts. Storage can also move the tier. A solo cleaner who does the work is one provider, which fits on Starter. The page says prices exclude sales tax where BookingKoala has to collect it.

**Scheduling and quotes.** Starter includes booking forms, one-step and two-step, smart scheduling, and a calendar in vertical and list views. The form tooltip says you and the customer can book, and the customer sees pricing and availability you set. A separate lead form, for a quote request that is not the booking form, is also on Starter. Horizontal and map calendar views, GPS, and clock-in are on Growing. Multi-step forms are on Premium. The [recurring-invoice article](https://help.bookingkoala.com/help/recurring-invoice-q-and-as) describes bookings on a weekly and every-two-week frequency, and it says a customer can have one recurring booking-invoice schedule. **Invoicing and payments.** The comparison table marks invoicing, and charging and refunding cards, on Starter. **Phone and texts.** The comparison table marks mobile access, meaning the account on a phone, tablet, or computer, on Starter. The mobile-apps row is on Growing. Email notifications are on Starter. SMS notifications are on Growing. The Growing card says there are over 100 notification types.

The FAQ says the card is charged when the 14-day trial ends, and that you can cancel anytime, with no contract. The heading on the same page also reads "14 30-Days." See unverified claims. There is no setup fee for ordinary help. The page says a full store setup is referred to a third party.

## Jobber

Jobber's [cleaning page](https://www.getjobber.com/industries/cleaning-business-software/) describes quotes, a schedule of one-time and recurring cleans, invoices, and a client hub. It does not name Core, Connect, or Grow. The plan articles and the pricing table do. [Core](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-core-plan/), updated November 18, 2026, is the one-person plan: one user, and an unlimited number of quotes, jobs, and invoices. [Connect](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-connect-plan/), updated the same day, is where automatic payments, routing, automated reminders, and QuickBooks Online are named. The Connect article says the plan supports up to five users.

**Scheduling.** Core includes requests, online booking, and the calendar. A request form can sit on a website or a social page. A job is one-off or recurring. [Job basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/job-basics/), updated November 12, 2026, uses weekly cleaning as the recurring example, and a move-out clean as the one-off example. A one-off job can hold up to 20 visits and still is not a repeating schedule. The same article says the job type cannot be switched later. A one-off deep clean cannot be turned into the weekly clean. Recreate it as a recurring job.

[Recurring jobs](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/create-a-recurring-job/), updated November 12, 2026, repeat weekly, biweekly, monthly, or on a custom pattern, including two days a week or the first and third Friday. Billing is per visit, or a fixed price for the period, such as one amount per month. Jobber's own guidance is to end recurring jobs within a few years, because a very long schedule creates enough visits to slow the account down. A single visit can carry different line items, and those lines transfer to a per-visit invoice. On a fixed-price job they do not transfer.

The pricing table marks automated client notifications on Connect, not on Core. The Core article says on-my-way texts are not on Core. The Connect article describes automated appointment reminders. Routing, including assigning a job to the closest person on the map, is in the Connect article. A solo cleaner who already knows the order of the houses is on the Core calendar.

**Quotes.** Core quotes use your branding. There is no cap on how many you send, and the customer can approve them online. The Core article says optional line items are not on Core. The pricing table marks optional add-ons and markups on Grow. That is the cleaning version of "standard clean, or standard clean plus the fridge and the inside of the oven" on one quote. The cleaning page describes that choice and does not name Grow.

**Invoicing and payments.** On Core, an invoice is created from the job and the customer can pay it online. The pricing table marks progress invoicing on Core. Connect adds automatic reminders for unpaid invoices and for quotes that have not been approved.

Jobber Payments is on Core in the US, Canada, and the UK. The [pricing table](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/) shows the same three rates on Core, Connect, Grow, and Plus: cards at 2.9% + 30¢, Tap to Pay on the phone at 2.7% + 30¢, and US bank payments (ACH) at 1%. The table shows one card rate. It does not publish a separate rate for American Express or for a card kept on file. The Core article says instant payouts to a debit card are available for an extra 1%. Customers can add a tip when they pay online.

[Automatic payments](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/automatic-payments/) charge a card or ACH account saved through Jobber Payments when a recurring job's invoice is created. The Connect article names that feature. The pricing page says credit and debit card processing rates apply.

Consumer financing is [Wisetack](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/jobber-and-wisetack-consumer-financing-integration/), a flat 3.9% to the contractor, on every plan except Lite, United States only. The article, updated October 29, 2026, lists the industries that are not eligible: pool and spa, residential cleaning, commercial cleaning, window washing, and snow removal. A house cleaning business is on that list. The repeating-schedule rule would have excluded the weekly clean anyway. The industry rule excludes the one-off deep clean as well.

**QuickBooks.** The pricing table names "QuickBooks Online and Xero sync" on Connect and up. The [QuickBooks Online FAQ](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/quickbooks-integration-faqs-new-quickbooks-integration/), updated October 29, 2026, says the new sync is rolling out to select accounts. It runs one way, Jobber to QuickBooks Online. Clients, products and services, invoices, payments (including payouts, tips, and refunds), and timesheets sync. Paid status no longer comes back from QuickBooks into Jobber. The first connection can import clients and products from QuickBooks once. After that, edit those records in Jobber. Supported US versions named in that FAQ are Simple Start, Essentials, Plus, and Advanced. QuickBooks Desktop does not sync. The FAQ does not describe Xero. See unverified claims.

The pricing page lists Jobber AI Receptionist at $99 per month. The Core and Connect articles call it an add-on. The Plus card includes an AI receptionist. Plus starts at five users. A solo cleaner who wants the phone answered is looking at the $99 add-on, not at Plus.

## Housecall Pro

For a cleaning business, the product on the [pricing page](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/) is the standard plan. The [maid service page](https://www.housecallpro.com/industries/maid-service-software/) describes recurring cleans, online booking, invoices, and payment. It does not list plan prices, and it does not say Basic, Essentials, or MAX. ## What to watch out for

Count the limit the plan actually uses. ZenMaid Starter counts appointments in the month, and it stops at 40. FreshBooks counts clients, and archived clients count. Lite's 5 includes a house you cleaned once and might clean again, until you delete the record. Plus, at 50, is the plan to budget if the list has to stay. Jobber Core's article says quotes, jobs, and invoices are unlimited, and the limit there is one user. BookingKoala counts providers, the people who do the cleaning, or storage, whichever tier you reach first. Square Free's calendar tooltip says there is no employee limit, and the subscription is $0.

A Jobber user is someone who opens the schedule. The owner on ZenMaid is included. A cleaner profile, a contractor profile, or an office manager on ZenMaid is the extra charge in the cost article. A helper who rides along and never logs in is not a Jobber user and is not a ZenMaid profile. A helper who needs the day's list on a phone is.

Build the repeat clean in the shape the product stores. In Jobber, create a recurring job at the start. A one-off job will not convert later, and automatic payment of that series is named on Connect. In ZenMaid, each weekday can be its own recurring series, invoices are created by hand, and a repeating card charge is set up in Stripe or Square, not by ZenMaid. In Housecall Pro, a repeating job is a recurring job, and the article never calls that tool MAX. A service plan stops at 12 visits a year, and buying MAX to "get recurring" buys the membership. In FreshBooks, a recurring template sends the invoice. The clean itself is still a date you keep. In Square, recurring invoices are on the free plan. The scheduling article says appointments can recur, and the steps fetched for this guide do not show the control.

Count the card rate you will actually pay. A card tapped on the phone is Square's 2.6% + 15¢ on Free, Jobber's 2.7% + 30¢, or Housecall Pro's 2.59% plus $5 in a month that phone takes one of those payments. A card the customer pays on an invoice link is Square's 3.3% + 30¢, Jobber's 2.9% + 30¢, Housecall Pro's 2.99%, or FreshBooks' 2.9% + $0.30 for a standard consumer card. A saved card charged by the business is the rate to check before you turn on automatic billing: Square publishes 3.5% + 15¢, Housecall Pro publishes 3.49%, and FreshBooks publishes 3.5% + $0.30 for card AutoPay and for the virtual terminal. Jobber's pricing page points at the published card rate and does not list a second one. ZenMaid and BookingKoala do not publish a rate. Theirs is the Stripe, Square, or other processor account you connect. American Express and business cards are called out at the higher rate by Housecall Pro and by FreshBooks. Square and Jobber's pricing table each show one card rate.

Jobber's $29 and $99 prices are a one-year term. The no-commitment prices are $49 and $139. Housecall Pro's $59 Basic price is the annual figure on the card. The month-to-month figure next to it is $79, and the FAQ says there is no long-term contract. ZenMaid's $19 and BookingKoala's $27 are the month-to-month prices those pages print. FreshBooks' $464.40 is the yearly Plus price, against $43 paid each month. The 90% and 50% offers are promotions. Budget the regular price.

There is no cleaning package that moves Housecall Pro's MAX tools onto a cheaper card. Memberships and the visual proposal stay on MAX. QuickBooks stays on Essentials. Jobber's Wisetack article excludes residential and commercial cleaning, so a financing offer on the quote is not a reason to pick Jobber for this trade.

QuickBooks will drift if the same customers are edited in both products. Jobber's current Online sync is one way, out of Jobber, and Jobber says that version is still reaching select accounts. Jobber does not sync QuickBooks Desktop. Housecall Pro's Online article also describes a feed that typically runs one way, into QuickBooks, with a manual push when an invoice changes. ZenMaid's pricing card says the QuickBooks integration is coming soon. FreshBooks' documented path on the comparison table is Easy Switch on Select.

The cleaning pages lead with results: pros on the platform, hours saved, revenue growth. Those are the vendors' aggregates. Nothing on this page comes from our using any of these apps on a house.

ZenMaid Starter, until the month hits 40 appointments. Jobber Core, as a recurring job, weekly or every other week, with no appointment cap in the Core article.
