A one-to-three-truck HVAC company is usually choosing software for the same kind of week: a no-cool call late in the day, a replacement quote at the kitchen table, and maintenance agreements that should bill themselves in spring and fall. This page compares [Jobber](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/) and [Housecall Pro](https://www.housecallpro.com/industries/hvac-software/) on the parts of that week. It is a comparison of the vendors' pricing pages and help centers, checked on November 18, 2026. We have not run either product on a job.

## Who it's for

Use this if you run residential or light-commercial HVAC with one, two, or three trucks. That is the owner still on the phone, plus a few techs who need the day's schedule on their own phones. It also fits a solo tech who writes the invoice in the driveway.

It is not a comparison of enterprise HVAC systems. Both companies sell a top tier, Jobber Plus and Housecall Pro MAX, aimed at larger crews. Those tiers are in the pricing section so you can see the price, and then ignore them unless you specifically need route optimization, a sales pipeline, or an included receptionist.

## Quick answer

If you sell maintenance agreements and system replacements, Housecall Pro's HVAC Essentials plan is the closer match. On that plan the HVAC package includes memberships, equipment history on the property, flat-rate pricing, price-book commissions, job costing, QuickBooks Online, and a visual sales proposal. The team price on the HVAC page is **$299 per month with five users included**. The same card shows a three-month promotion at $149.50, then $299. Checked November 18, 2026.

Jobber is the lower ongoing bill, and it is the one that has a real one-person plan. Core is a single user at **$29 per month billed annually**, or **$49 month-to-month**. A shop that needs several logins, QuickBooks Online, and automatic payment on repeat visits moves to Connect: five users at **$149 per month billed annually**, or **$199 month-to-month**. Grow is the step up for package-style quotes, job costing, and two-way texting: five users at **$229 billed annually**, or **$299 month-to-month**.

The subscription is not the whole cost. Housecall Pro's published rate for a saved card is 3.49%, and a saved card is how its maintenance plans get paid. Jobber publishes 2.9% + 30¢ for cards and does not list a separate saved-card rate. Rates are in the payments notes below.

## Jobber

Jobber covers quoting, scheduling, invoicing, and taking payment. [Core](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-core-plan/) is the one-person plan. [Connect](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-connect-plan/) is where a small crew, QuickBooks Online, and automatic payments show up. [Grow](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-grow-plan/) is where a quote can offer choices and where job profit is visible.

**Scheduling and dispatch.** Core includes the calendar, online booking, and requests that become a quote or a job. A job is either one-off or recurring. The Connect plan article adds routing: route the day's work, and assign an incoming job to the closest person on the map. That article also lists live vehicle GPS through FleetSharp. [Jobber's FleetSharp article](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/fleetsharp-tracker-devices/) says the devices are purchased separately, on a FleetSharp contract, so the hardware is not included in the Jobber price. Phone waypoint tracking, a pin when someone clocks in or completes a visit, is documented as available on select plans. The plan names are not in that article.

A Jobber product update from October 24, 2025 said route optimization was on Core, Connect, Grow, and Plus. The Core plan article, updated October 28, 2026, does not list routing. Treat route optimization on Core as unverified.

Core also does not include "on my way" texts or optional line items. The Core article says both are unavailable on that plan.

**Quotes.** Core quotes carry your branding, there is no cap on how many you send, and the customer can approve them online. Grow adds [optional line items](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/optional-line-items-on-quotes/), which you can use as add-ons or as packages the customer picks between, plus images, extra text, and markups that show an estimated margin. That is how a repair-versus-replace choice is built in Jobber. It is still a quote, not a separate proposal document. Line items come from Jobber's products and services list.

**Invoicing.** On Core, an invoice is created from the job and the customer can pay it online. Connect adds automatic reminders for unpaid invoices and for quotes that have not been approved. [Payment schedules](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/progress-invoicing/) can split one job into a deposit and later invoices. That article does not name a required plan.

**Payments.** Jobber Payments is on Core in the US, Canada, and the UK. The [pricing table](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/) shows the same three rates on Core, Connect, Grow, and Plus: cards at 2.9% + 30¢, Tap to Pay on the phone at 2.7% + 30¢, and US bank payments (ACH) at 1%. The Core article says instant payouts to a debit card are available on Core for an extra 1%. Customers can add a tip when they pay online. Automatic payments, which charge a card or bank account saved through Jobber Payments, are listed on the Connect plan and work on recurring jobs only.

Consumer financing is [Wisetack](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/jobber-and-wisetack-consumer-financing-integration/). Jobber's help center says it is on every plan except Lite, costs the contractor a flat 3.9%, and is available only in the United States. The quote has to be a one-off residential job, in a person's name rather than a company name, titled, between $500 and $65,000, and completed within 90 days. Repeating work is excluded. Wisetack's approval notes in that article ask for a review history (about 10 reviews averaging near 4.5 stars) and more than $12,500 a month or $150,000 a year in revenue. A new company can be turned down. The industries that article lists as ineligible are pool and spa, residential and commercial cleaning, window washing, and snow removal. HVAC is not on that list. Approval is still Wisetack's decision. Funds arrive 1–3 business days after the customer confirms the work is done.

**Maintenance agreements.** Jobber's version of a tune-up contract is a [recurring job](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/create-a-recurring-job/), and the Core workflow includes recurring jobs. You set the visit schedule, then bill per visit or a fixed price for the period, such as one amount per month no matter how many visits fell in it. Invoices can be created on the last day of the month, after each visit is completed, on a custom schedule, or when the job is closed. On Connect and above, [automatic payments](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/automatic-payments/) charge the saved card or ACH account when that invoice is created. Two limits are explicit. A one-off job cannot be turned into a recurring job later; [Jobber says to recreate it](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/job-basics/). And there is no membership you attach to a replacement quote for the customer to accept with the same signature. Shops that sell the agreement on the replacement proposal are the ones who feel that gap.

**QuickBooks.** Connect includes QuickBooks Online sync. The current integration is [one-way, from Jobber to QuickBooks Online](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/quickbooks-integration-faqs-new-quickbooks-integration/), and it keeps running. Clients, products and services, invoices, and payments sync, including payouts, tips, and refunds. Timesheets sync after they are approved in Jobber, and only if QuickBooks Payroll is on and the employee names match. Paid status no longer comes back from QuickBooks into Jobber. The first connection can import clients and products from QuickBooks into Jobber once. After that, Jobber's help center says to edit those records in Jobber. [QuickBooks Desktop does not sync.](https://www.getjobber.com/features/quickbooks-sync/) A CSV of Desktop clients is a one-time file import.

**Mobile app.** The iOS and Android apps are how that one Core user, or each paid user on a higher plan, sees the schedule and takes payment. Jobber's pricing FAQ says the mobile app is available in Spanish for team members, and not for administrators or the account owner.

## Housecall Pro

For this shop the product is the [HVAC package](https://www.housecallpro.com/industries/hvac-software/), not the generic Housecall Pro plan. The [package overview](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/15935808-hvac-package-overview) says you get a standard Housecall Pro plan plus HVAC tools, and that memberships and the sales proposal tool come with Essentials on this package. The generic pricing page puts both of those on MAX. Confirm the HVAC package before you pay.

**Scheduling and dispatch.** HVAC Basic includes scheduling, dispatch, and online booking, including booking from Google or the company website. That is a one-person schedule. Route optimization, which reorders the day from drive time and live traffic, is listed on HVAC MAX only. [Vehicle GPS hardware](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/6060351-vehicle-gps-tracking-overview) is a separate add-on at $20 per vehicle per month, with no contract. It is not included in the plan price.

**Quotes.** Basic sends estimates. Essentials adds the sales proposal tool: visual proposals with images and side-by-side comparisons. On the HVAC page, Housecall Pro describes that tool as good, better, and best options on installs and changeouts, and says a maintenance agreement can be added on the same proposal. The generic pricing page lists the same tool on MAX and calls it a "$40/mo value," which is the practical reason to sign up on the HVAC package rather than the generic one.

**Invoicing.** Basic includes estimates and invoicing, and the customer can pay at any hour. Housecall Pro's HVAC FAQ says commercial-style billing includes progress invoices on large jobs and batch invoices for recurring accounts. It does not say which plan includes progress invoices.

**Payments.** Rates below are from Housecall Pro's [payments help center](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/2046930-housecall-pro-payment-processing-options/).

- Card swiped, tapped, chipped, or taken with Tap to Pay: 2.59%.
- Tap to Pay also costs [$5 per active device](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/8279161-getting-started-with-tap-to-pay-on-mobile/) in any month that device accepts a Tap to Pay payment.
- Card entered by the customer on an invoice: 2.99%.
- Card typed by staff, scanned in the app, or saved on file: 3.49%.
- American Express, and commercial, corporate, or business cards, any method: 3.49%.
- ACH: 1%.
- Consumer financing: starting at 3.9%. HVAC Basic lists payments and consumer financing. The approval rules for that program were not on the pages checked.
- Mobile check deposit: the pricing FAQ says depositing a check in the app has no fee. The payments article lists a 1–4 business day payout and no percentage for that method.
- Instant payout (Instapay) adds 1%.

The HVAC package includes credit-card surcharging with no extra Housecall Pro fee, starting on Basic. The help center describes it as a compliant surcharge that passes the processing cost to the customer. Whether you may surcharge depends on your state and the card brand. Check that before turning it on.

**Maintenance agreements.** On the HVAC package, membership plans start at Essentials. The package overview says you sell one on an estimate or a job, with one signature and one payment. The [service-plan FAQ](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/5485025-service-plans-faqs/) describes them as subscriptions: if the plan runs until the customer cancels, the card on file is charged on each billing date and the customer gets an email receipt. Billing frequency cannot be changed after acceptance. You cancel the plan and send a new one. If the customer pays by cash or check and does not pay the whole plan up front, Housecall Pro does not collect those installments for you. They show as due. A card kept on file for a service plan is a saved card, so the published rate is [3.49%](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/6570769-card-on-file/), not the 2.59% in-person rate. Service-plan payments are included in what syncs to QuickBooks.

Basic does not include memberships. It is the wrong plan if agreements are a real share of revenue.

**QuickBooks.** HVAC Essentials and MAX include QuickBooks Online. The price card describes it as an import of existing QuickBooks data. The [onboarding guide](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/3006688-quickbooks-online-integration-onboarding-guide) is narrower and more useful: you can import customers, tax rates, invoices, products, services, income accounts, and classes (classes become business units). Only the parent customer and one level of sub-customer come across. Deeper sub-customers, and their invoices, do not. You must be on Housecall Pro Essentials or MAX to connect. After that, the [sync article](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/6293215-quickbooks-online-syncing-information-from-housecall-pro) says the integration typically runs one way, from Housecall Pro to QuickBooks Online: invoices, payments (including service-plan payments), new customers, and new price-book items. The same article says an update made in Housecall Pro may still need a manual push before QuickBooks refreshes. Edit in one system.

The English onboarding guide, dated March 5, 2026, lists QuickBooks Online Simple Start, Essentials, Plus, and Advanced as supported. The Spanish version of that guide, dated January 21, 2026, says the integration is not compatible with Simple Start. Those pages disagree. See unverified claims.

Housecall Pro also documents [QuickBooks Desktop](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/4333056-quickbooks-desktop-syncing-information-from-housecall-pro). After the initial import, that sync is one-way, from Housecall Pro to Desktop, through Intuit's Web Connector. Jobs push when they are finished, invoiced, or marked paid, along with the customer, address, line items, tax, and payments. Payments land in Undeposited Funds, and card-processing fees need a journal entry you make yourself. The connector can run on a timer or you can push it by hand. Housecall Pro warns that auto-run plus multi-user mode in Desktop can lose data, and says to sync by hand in that case. Requirements in the [setup guide](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/4327479-quickbooks-desktop-integration-onboarding-guide) (February 4, 2026): a US edition of Desktop, not older than three years, on Windows. That guide does not say whether Desktop is included on Basic, Essentials, or MAX.

**Equipment, flat rate, and job costing.** These sit on HVAC Essentials with the membership and the proposal, which is why a two-truck shop often lands here even when Basic would cover the calendar. Equipment tracking keeps a service history on the property. Flat-rate pricing builds margin into the service price. Commissions can be set on price-book items. Job costing tracks cost on the job. The package overview also puts purchase orders, an HVAC KPI dashboard, and the no-fee surcharge on Basic.

**Mobile app.** Every plan includes the iOS and Android app, in English and Spanish. The generic pricing page lists offline viewing on every plan. Housecall Pro's HVAC page says the apps are rated 4.6 on the Apple App Store and 4.6 on Google Play. That figure is Housecall Pro's, and we did not check the stores.

### What a 1–3 truck shop actually compares

For an owner plus two or three techs, the shelf prices to put next to each other are:

- Jobber Connect, 5 users: $199 month-to-month, or $149 billed annually, with a one-year term on the annual price.
- Jobber Grow, 5 users: $299 month-to-month, or $229 billed annually, same term.
- Housecall Pro HVAC Essentials, team: $299 with five users included and no stated term, or $149.50 for the first three months printed on the card.

At the month-to-month price, Essentials costs the same as Grow and includes memberships, equipment history, flat-rate pricing, and the visual proposal. Grow's annual price is lower if a recurring job is enough and you will stay the year. Basic and Core are one-user plans. They are the right comparison only for a solo operator.

## What to watch out for

Count logins, not trucks. A helper who never opens the app may not need a seat. A tech who needs the schedule does. Jobber Core and Housecall Pro HVAC Basic are both one user. Running three trucks on either one means someone is still dispatching by text.

Buy Housecall Pro's HVAC package on purpose. On the generic pricing page, recurring service plans and the sales proposal tool are MAX features. On the HVAC package they are Essentials features. The logo is the same.

Do not compare Jobber's annual price with Housecall Pro's cancel-anytime price and call it the same kind of commitment. Jobber's $149 and $229 five-user prices are a one-year term. The no-commitment Connect and Grow prices for five users are $199 and $299.

Build the maintenance agreement in the shape the product actually stores. In Jobber, create a recurring job at the start; a one-off job will not convert later. In Housecall Pro, set the billing frequency before the customer accepts, and expect a saved-card charge at 3.49% unless a surcharge is allowed where you work.

QuickBooks will drift if you edit the same customers in both products. Jobber does not sync QuickBooks Desktop at all. Housecall Pro's Desktop sync is one-way, through the Web Connector, and it needs a Windows PC running a recent US edition of Desktop. The setup guide does not name the Housecall Pro plan that includes it.

Both sites lead with results: faster payment, hours saved, revenue growth. Those are the vendors' aggregates. Nothing on this page comes from our using either app in the field.

## Claims we could not verify

- **Route optimization on Jobber Core.** The October 24, 2025 product update says it is on Core, Connect, Grow, and Plus. The Core plan article, updated October 28, 2026, does not list routing. The Connect article, updated November 9, 2026, does. If optimized routes are a requirement, confirm them inside a Core trial or budget Connect.

- **Which Jobber plan includes phone GPS waypoints, and which includes progress invoicing.** Waypoints are "select plans." The progress-invoicing article does not name a plan.
- **Which Housecall Pro plan includes progress invoicing.** The HVAC page describes staging invoices on a changeout and does not name a tier.
- **Whether Jobber automatic payments use the published 2.9% + 30¢ card rate.** The pricing table has one card rate. It does not list a separate card-on-file rate.
- **Housecall Pro's annual price as a different number from the monthly price.** The billing-cycle fields in the HVAC page source are `$XX`. The table uses the individual and team prices that were filled in.
- **Individual Housecall Pro MAX at $399.** The July 2026 FAQ says $399. The card on November 18, 2026 says $349.
- **Which Housecall Pro plan includes QuickBooks Desktop.** The one-way Desktop sync is documented. The setup guide dated February 4, 2026 does not name Basic, Essentials, or MAX.
- **QuickBooks Online Simple Start with Housecall Pro.** The English onboarding guide, dated March 5, 2026, lists Simple Start as supported. The Spanish version, dated January 21, 2026, says Simple Start is not compatible. A shop on Simple Start should confirm with Housecall Pro before buying a plan for the sync.
- **A Jobber record for the furnace and condenser on a property** (model, serial, age), comparable to Housecall Pro equipment tracking. We did not find one on the plan or help pages read for this guide. Connect has custom fields, which can hold notes you define. That is not an equipment history.
- **Housecall Pro consumer-financing approval rules,** beyond the published "starting at 3.9%."
- **Jobber's user caps.** The Connect help article says the plan supports up to 5 users. The Grow article says up to 10. The pricing page sells 10- and 15-user packs. The 1-user and 5-user prices are what this shop needs; the higher packs were not reconciled.

## FAQ

**We are one owner and one truck. What is the least we can pay?**

Jobber Core, if one login is enough: $29 per month billed annually, or $49 with no commitment. That includes scheduling, quotes, invoices, and online card payments. It does not include QuickBooks Online sync, automatic charging of repeat visits, or optional line items. Housecall Pro HVAC Basic is $79 per month for one user, or $39.50 for the first three months on the card checked November 18, 2026. Basic adds a price book, fee-free surcharging, and a KPI dashboard. It does not include membership plans.

**Three people need the app. What bill should we expect?**

Use a five-user plan. Jobber Connect is $199 month-to-month or $149 billed annually. Jobber Grow is $299 or $229 on those same terms. Housecall Pro HVAC Essentials for a team is $299 with five users included, or $149.50 for three months on the card, and a sixth user is $100 more. If those three people sell maintenance agreements, Essentials is the plan that includes them.

**Can the tech take a card in the driveway?**

Yes in both, from the entry plan, in the mobile app. Jobber's published Tap to Pay rate is 2.7% + 30¢. Housecall Pro's in-person and Tap to Pay rate is 2.59%, plus $5 in a month when that phone takes a Tap to Pay payment. A card typed into Housecall Pro, or saved on file, is 3.49%.

**We use QuickBooks Desktop. Will either product keep syncing?**

Jobber will not. Its integration is QuickBooks Online only, and a Desktop client list can be brought in once from a CSV. Housecall Pro documents a one-way sync from Housecall Pro into QuickBooks Desktop through Intuit's Web Connector, after an import the other direction when you connect. That requires a US Desktop edition from the last three years, on Windows. It is not a live two-way link, and the setup guide does not say which Housecall Pro plan includes it.

**Does either product replace QuickBooks?**

No. Both run the jobs. QuickBooks stays the books. For QuickBooks Online, the feed is one-way once you are on Jobber Connect or Housecall Pro Essentials. Someone still reconciles.

**We want good, better, and best on a system replacement.**

Housecall Pro includes that proposal on HVAC Essentials, and a membership can be sold on it. Jobber's version is optional line items on Grow, which the customer picks between inside the quote. Grow does not attach a membership to that quote.
