A residential maid service that wants online booking is usually buying software for a specific week: a new customer opens a form, sees a price for the house, picks a weekly or every-other-week clean, leaves a card, and the card is charged after the team leaves, without a phone call. This page compares [ZenMaid](https://get.zenmaid.com/pricing) and [BookingKoala](https://www.bookingkoala.com/pricing/) on that work for a small house-cleaning or maid service. It is a comparison of the vendors' pricing pages and help centers, checked on November 25, 2026. We have not run either product on a house.

A one-person cleaner who is also comparing Square, Jobber, Housecall Pro, or FreshBooks should use the [solo house cleaning guide]({{ '/guides/best-software-solo-house-cleaning-business/' | relative_url }}). The repeating calendar, cleaner checklists, and payroll, with ZenMaid next to Jobber, are the [ZenMaid vs Jobber guide]({{ '/guides/zenmaid-vs-jobber-recurring-maid-service/' | relative_url }}). A company choosing between Jobber and Housecall Pro should use the [residential cleaning comparison]({{ '/guides/jobber-vs-housecall-pro-residential-cleaning/' | relative_url }}). This page stays with ZenMaid and BookingKoala, and with the booking form and the charge after the job.

## Who it's for

Use this if you run residential house cleaning or a maid service, you are the owner who still takes the booking, and the thing you are deciding is whether the customer can book on a form and be charged without you opening the appointment. That includes an owner who cleans alone and a small team whose cleaners each count as a person on the plan.

It is not a commercial janitorial system. It does not cover Square, Jobber, Housecall Pro, or FreshBooks except to point at the guides above. ZenMaid's pricing page is written for a maid service. BookingKoala's pricing page uses a maid as one example of a provider and sells the same plans to other appointment businesses.

## Quick answer

Prices below are the ones to budget. The pricing section has the date, the billing term, and the page each number came from. Neither pricing page prints an annual dollar amount.

**The customer books on your site, sees a price, and the software charges the card after the job.** [BookingKoala Starter](https://www.bookingkoala.com/pricing/) is **$27 per month**, with 5 providers or 5 GB. A provider is someone who performs the service, and the page's example is a maid. Starter includes one-step and two-step booking forms, a lead form for quote requests, smart scheduling, invoicing, and charging and refunding cards. The [payments article](https://help.bookingkoala.com/help/setting-up-the-payment-section-under-the-store-options-general-tab), updated September 28, 2026, describes an automatic charge after the booking is done. That article does not name Starter, Growing, or Premium. The pricing table checks the charge-and-refund row on Starter. BookingKoala does not add a processing fee of its own. The processor is Stripe, Square, PayPal through Braintree, or Authorize.net, one at a time. Text messages, the native apps, and removal of BookingKoala branding are on Growing at **$57 per month**, with 15 providers or 15 GB.

**A maid-service calendar, a booking form, and a card you charge yourself.** [ZenMaid Pro](https://get.zenmaid.com/pricing) is **$39 per month**. Pro lists booking forms with ZenMaid branding, unlimited appointments, and online payments through a Stripe or Square account you connect. Pro Max, at **$49 per month**, is the card that lists the same forms with your own branding. Starter is **$19 per month** and includes scheduling, invoicing, and Stripe or Square payments, and it stops at 40 appointments in that month. Starter's card does not list booking forms. The [charge article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/12099721-ways-customers-can-be-charged-by-card-in-zenmaid), dated January 30, 2026, says invoices are created by hand, and that marking an appointment paid does not charge the card. The [processor article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/4801218-connecting-a-credit-card-processor-to-zenmaid), dated January 29, 2026, says a repeating or automatic charge is configured in Stripe or Square. ZenMaid does not start it. ZenMaid does not publish a card rate, and its [payments page](https://get.zenmaid.com/credit-card-processing) says it adds no fee on top of Stripe or Square.

Buy BookingKoala Starter if the priced booking page and a charge after the scheduled job are the product, and the crew is five providers or fewer. Buy Growing if those cleaners need texts, the native app, or a form without BookingKoala branding. Buy ZenMaid Pro if you want the cleaning calendar and the form, and you will charge the saved card from the appointment or let Stripe or Square run the repeating charge. Buy Starter only if the book is still under 40 appointments in the month and the customer-facing form can wait.

## Choose BookingKoala if

Choose BookingKoala if the customer has to see a price and a time on the form, and the card has to be charged after the job without a separate step in Stripe or Square.

Starter is the card that lists booking forms, smart scheduling, and charging cards. The payments article lets you turn on an automatic charge for the services you check, immediately after the scheduled length has passed, a set number of hours later, or the next day at a time you set. A separate [card-hold article](https://help.bookingkoala.com/help/setting-up-the-credit-card-hold-section-under-the-store-options-general-tab), updated the same day, places a hold before the job. That article does not name a plan either. The [processor overview](https://help.bookingkoala.com/help/payment-processors-overview), updated November 20, 2024, says the subscription covers the connection and BookingKoala adds no transaction fee.

Choose BookingKoala if five people who clean fit on one bill. Starter includes 5 providers. A sixth provider does not upgrade the account by itself. The store stays up, and adding that provider asks you to change plans. Growing, at $57, includes 15 providers and is the card that adds SMS, the mobile apps, GPS and clock-in, and removal of BookingKoala branding.

Choose BookingKoala if you will stay month to month. The FAQ says there is no contract and you can cancel anytime. The same FAQ says the card is charged when the 14-day trial ends. The heading on that page also reads "14 30-Days." See unverified claims.

## Choose ZenMaid if

Choose ZenMaid if the product you are buying is a maid-service calendar, and a charge you start from the appointment is enough.

Pro is the card that lists the booking form, at $39, with ZenMaid branding. The [pricing article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/3242732-how-to-price-your-booking-form), dated September 10, 2026, can show an estimated price on the form from bedroom, bathroom, square-footage, and hour sliders, plus a base price, a minimum, flat-fee extras, and a discount on a recurrence. The [time-slot article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/3518448-how-to-create-booking-form-time-slots-and-edit-bookable-days), dated July 3, 2026, says the form is not a live calendar and that you should tell the customer you will confirm the slot. A recurring request is converted to a subscription from the pending bookings list. The [charge article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/12099721-ways-customers-can-be-charged-by-card-in-zenmaid) then has you charge the saved card from the appointment, in bulk from unpaid appointments, or from the contact. A repeating charge lives in Stripe or Square.

Choose ZenMaid if the price list does not scale by a fixed step per bedroom or bathroom. The same pricing article says the sliders scale in even steps only, and that the alternative is to hide the price and send it yourself. The [quote article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/6278498-how-to-create-and-customize-an-estimate-or-quote-form-in-zenmaid), dated July 3, 2026, is that booking form with the prices shown or hidden. It does not name a plan.

Choose ZenMaid if you will stay month to month and the crew is priced as cleaner profiles on top of the owner. The pricing FAQ says there is no contract. The [cost article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/2423512-how-much-does-zenmaid-cost), dated June 4, 2026, says only the owner profile is included. Checklists, payroll, and cleaner GPS are on the Pro card. Those tools, next to Jobber, are the [recurring maid-service guide]({{ '/guides/zenmaid-vs-jobber-recurring-maid-service/' | relative_url }}).

## ZenMaid

ZenMaid's [pricing page](https://get.zenmaid.com/pricing) sells three month-to-month plans to a maid service. Starter is $19, Pro is $39, and Pro Max is $49. Each card says SMS charges are not included. The [cost article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/2423512-how-much-does-zenmaid-cost), dated June 4, 2026, says only the owner profile is included and every other profile is an extra charge. The same article says annual plans exist on request and does not print an annual price.

**Scheduling and the booking form.** Starter includes scheduling with calendar, dispatch, and map views, and it stops at 40 appointments in the month. The pricing FAQ says that at the cap you cannot add another appointment that month, and the account sends you to upgrade. Pro lists unlimited appointments. Ten weekly houses are 40 visits in a four-week month and 50 in a five-week month. That is arithmetic from the cap, not a package ZenMaid sells.

The customer-facing form is a different gate. The Pro card lists booking forms with ZenMaid branding. Pro Max lists the same forms with your branding. Starter's card does not list them. The [SMS article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/370973-sms-templates-in-zenmaid), dated October 23, 2026, says the booking form is included in the paid plan and can be placed on your website. Starter is a paid plan. See unverified claims.

The [time-slot article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/3518448-how-to-create-booking-form-time-slots-and-edit-bookable-days), dated July 3, 2026, says the form is not a live calendar. The suggested wording is that you will confirm the slot the customer picked. The form can offer a recurrence. The [manage-bookings article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/3417682-how-to-manage-your-bookings), dated July 3, 2026, puts a new request in a Pending tab. A one-time request converts to an appointment. A recurring request, the article's examples are monthly and bi-weekly, converts to a subscription, and the appointments then appear on the calendar at that frequency. A request with no date converts to a lead. Confirmed bookings cannot be deleted. The [color-code article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/3620974-how-to-color-code-the-appointments-on-the-calendar), dated April 22, 2026, is the list of frequencies the calendar already knows: weekly, bi-weekly, every three weeks, monthly or every four weeks, and one-time. None of those articles name Starter, Pro, or Pro Max. Spotfinder, "easy scheduling of new recurring customers," is on the Pro card.

**Quotes.** The [pricing article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/3242732-how-to-price-your-booking-form), dated September 10, 2026, is the instant price. Bedroom, bathroom, square-footage, and man-hour sliders scale by a fixed step. A base price is added to every estimate. A minimum overrides any total below it. Extras are flat fees, not percentages. A recurrence option on the form can carry a discount. Sales tax follows the default on job estimations, or you set no tax. "Show Pricing on Form" is the switch for the running estimate. If it is off, the price is still saved with the booking, and the confirmation email can include it if that email is turned on for the form. The article says a price list that does not scale evenly has to be changed to fit the sliders, or the form collects the details and you send the price. The form does not support dropdown options. The [quote article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/6278498-how-to-create-and-customize-an-estimate-or-quote-form-in-zenmaid), dated July 3, 2026, renames that form as an estimate, with prices shown or hidden. It does not name a plan.

**Invoicing and payments.** Starter includes invoicing and online payments with Stripe and Square. The [charge article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/12099721-ways-customers-can-be-charged-by-card-in-zenmaid), dated January 30, 2026, says a card has to be saved on the customer before ZenMaid can charge it, and that marking an appointment paid does not charge the card. From the appointment you can charge the card on file and enter the amount. From the list of unpaid appointments you can charge several cards at once. An appointment with a saved card shows a card icon. The bulk action is unavailable if one selected appointment has no card, or if the processor is not connected. From the contact's billing tab you can charge an amount you enter. For a weekly or monthly clean, the same article says you charge each appointment, charge the unpaid invoices on one day, or put the month's total on one appointment in the series. Invoices are created by hand. When the customer pays the invoice, ZenMaid marks the linked appointment paid. An appointment paid by card cannot be edited or marked unpaid from that screen.

The [processor article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/4801218-connecting-a-credit-card-processor-to-zenmaid), dated January 29, 2026, says the account has to be Stripe or Square directly. A third party that hands you Stripe API keys is not supported. Recurring or automatic charges are configured in Stripe or Square. ZenMaid does not start them. Stripe and Square do not pre-authorize the card. They check that it is valid at the charge. Cards already stored in Stripe before the connection cannot be charged through ZenMaid. You collect the card again, with an invoice or a ZenMaid card authorization. Square has an import for cards already saved there. A payment-info element can sit on the booking form, and a Pay Now button is added to invoices once the processor is connected.

The [payments page](https://get.zenmaid.com/credit-card-processing) says ZenMaid adds no fee on top of Stripe or Square, and that it does not support deposits or pre-authorizations. The same page says a card the client uses to pay an invoice is stored on the profile, through Stripe or Square, not on ZenMaid's servers. ZenMaid does not publish the card rate. The rate is the processor's.

The [card-capture article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/3496266-how-to-use-the-booking-form-to-collect-an-existing-customer-s-credit-card) is a booking form stripped down to a card authorization, and the article says that version should not be used as a real booking. The email has to match the customer already on file. The card is not on the profile when the form is submitted. It is linked when you convert the pending booking. A failed transfer can still create the booking. The page, when this guide was checked, said it was updated over a month ago and did not print a calendar date.

**Accounting and the phone.** The Pro card lists QuickBooks integration and prints "COMING SOON" on that line. Pro Max lists Mailchimp and Zapier. The mobile app is on Starter without cleaner GPS. Pro adds GPS. The cost article says SMS bundles start at $14 for 125 messages. The pricing page says SMS is extra and does not print that bundle price. The SMS article says texts go out from a do-not-reply number, with a 156-character limit, and that the sender itself is included.

The trial is 14 days. The pricing FAQ says you can cancel the trial or the subscription from the account settings, and that the plan is month-to-month with no contract. The page does not say whether the trial asks for a card.

## BookingKoala

BookingKoala's [pricing page](https://www.bookingkoala.com/pricing/) is a booking site plus the schedule behind it. Starter starts at $27 per month, Growing at $57, and Premium at $197. Those starting prices are 5 providers or 5 GB, 15 providers or 15 GB, and 50 providers or 50 GB. A provider is someone who performs the service. The page's examples include a maid, and it says each person on a team counts. Storage can also move the tier. On Premium, campaign contacts can move it too. A solo cleaner who does the work is one provider, which fits on Starter. Five cleaners fit on Starter's included five. The page says prices exclude sales tax where BookingKoala has to collect it.

**Scheduling and the booking form.** Starter includes booking forms, one-step and two-step, smart scheduling, and a calendar in vertical and list views. The form tooltip says you and the customer can book, and the customer sees pricing and availability you set. Your own domain, a BookingKoala subdomain, and connecting the form to an existing site are checked on Starter. Horizontal and map calendar views are on Growing. Multi-step forms are on Premium. Removing BookingKoala branding is checked on Growing, not Starter. The customer dashboard, where a customer views appointments, is checked on Starter.

The [scheduling article](https://help.bookingkoala.com/help/setting-up-your-store-options-scheduling-tab), updated September 2, 2025, can show a time only when at least one provider is available, or show every slot whether or not a provider is free. Assignment can be manual or automatic. A provider can be added to the job automatically, or asked to accept it. The same article's recurring settings are three horizons. "Just one appointment" keeps a single visit on the calendar and adds the next one after that visit is completed, and it can also block a number of future spots. "One month of appointments" schedules up to 12 bookings. "Six months of appointments" is also a maximum of 12 bookings. The article's own weekly example under that six-month label is 12 weekly dates, from May 1 through July 17, not six months of weekly visits. A later process adds future bookings for a recurring service that has not been canceled or suspended. The article does not name Starter, Growing, or Premium. Smart scheduling is the line the pricing table places on Starter.

**Quotes.** A lead form, for a quote request that is not the booking form, is checked on Starter. The [pricing-parameters article](https://help.bookingkoala.com/help/setting-up-pricing-parameters-for-form-1), updated September 5, 2024, is the instant total on Form 1. Each variable has a price and a time, and the booking total adds the selected variables together. The article's cleaning example is $20 for one bathroom and $30 for two. An exclude parameter subtracts a price when the customer removes something. The article does not name a plan. The [frequency article](https://help.bookingkoala.com/help/setting-up-frequencies-for-every-form), updated November 5, 2024, can attach a fixed or percentage discount to a frequency, on the first visit or on every visit. If the discount applies to every visit, an optional setting charges the one-time price difference when the customer cancels after only the first appointment. The [send-a-quote article](https://help.bookingkoala.com/help/send-a-quote), updated September 30, 2024, saves a filled-in form as a quote, emails it or copies a link, sets an expiration, and can add a percent or dollar offer. A reminder email can go out up to 31 days later. The article does not name a plan. It says the customer can edit the quote only if that permission is on. Otherwise the customer can change contact details, the address, and notes.

**Recurring bookings.** The frequency article says you add at least one frequency, and that a recurring frequency schedules future services and holds the space indefinitely. It says the product does not offer frequencies such as the 5th of every month, or the 14th and the 28th. The menu of repeat intervals is on that page, and the list did not come through as text when this guide was checked. See unverified claims. The [recurring-invoice article](https://help.bookingkoala.com/help/recurring-invoice-q-and-as), updated May 31, 2024, names the booking frequencies it will invoice. The table includes every week, every 2 weeks, every 3 weeks, and every 4 weeks, plus daily patterns and every 5 through 12 weeks and every 24 weeks. A customer can have more than one of those booking frequencies. The same article says you can create only one recurring booking-invoice schedule per customer. It does not name a plan.

**Invoicing and payments.** The comparison table marks invoicing, and charging and refunding cards, on Starter. Premium's additions on the charge row are an add-payment feature and an add-charge feature. The page does not define those two lines. Premium's additions on the invoice are a card-collection link, tips, partial payments, sending invoices automatically by email or SMS, automatic reminders, and a contract signature. The [invoice overview](https://help.bookingkoala.com/help/invoice-module-overview), updated June 4, 2024, says the invoice module is included in all subscriptions, and it lists those same Premium limits, plus hiding the BookingKoala line at the bottom of the invoice. The [invoice article](https://help.bookingkoala.com/help/creating-customer-invoices), updated August 30, 2024, walks through a one-time or recurring invoice, from a booking or as a custom invoice. On a plan that is not Premium, the invoice link is sent by hand. Automatic email needs a verified sender address. Automatic SMS needs a Twilio integration. The article does not print Twilio's price. The recurring-invoice article says a customer gets at most one recurring booking-invoice schedule, and at most five invoice reminders.

Cancellation fees are checked on Starter. The [cancellation article](https://help.bookingkoala.com/help/how-do-cancelation-fees-work), updated April 24, 2024, can charge one fee or several, as a dollar amount or a percentage of the booking, after a time of day the day before, or a number of hours before the job. A single cancellation can skip the fee. Resuming a canceled booking does not refund the fee by itself.

The [payments article](https://help.bookingkoala.com/help/setting-up-the-payment-section-under-the-store-options-general-tab), updated September 28, 2026, is the automatic charge. You can leave it off and charge by hand, or turn it on for the service categories you check. A new category is not added to that list by itself. The charge can run when the booking is completed, a number of hours after that, or the next day at a clock time you set. ## What to watch out for

Count the limit the plan actually uses. ZenMaid Starter counts appointments in the month, and it stops at 40. A weekly house is one appointment each time it is on the calendar, and a five-week month is more appointments than a four-week month. BookingKoala counts providers, the people who do the cleaning, or storage, whichever tier you reach first. On Premium it can also count campaign contacts. The BookingKoala pricing page does not print a monthly visit cap. A cleaner who needs a login is a ZenMaid profile and a BookingKoala provider. An owner who cleans is one BookingKoala provider. On ZenMaid the owner profile is the one included login, and a cleaner profile is the extra charge in the cost article.

The booking form is not the same object in each product. ZenMaid's time-slot article says the form is not a live calendar and that you confirm the slot. The price, if you show it, scales in even steps, and extras are flat fees. BookingKoala can hide slots that no provider can take, and Form 1 adds the prices of the options the customer picked. BookingKoala's six-month recurring setting still stops at 12 bookings. A weekly house under that setting is 12 weeks of visits in the article's example, and later visits are added by the recurring process. ZenMaid's pending booking becomes a subscription only after you convert it. The calendar color key already includes weekly and bi-weekly. Those ZenMaid articles do not name the plan.

Count the charge you will actually run. ZenMaid does not start it. You charge the saved card, or Stripe or Square runs a repeating charge you configured there. Marking the visit paid does not charge the card. Invoices are created by hand. BookingKoala's automatic charge runs when the scheduled length has passed, not when a cleaner taps a finished button in any article checked here. A job that runs long or finishes early is still on that clock unless a page you have not used says otherwise. Checking cash/check in the automatic-charge settings records an outside payment. It does not charge the card. A new service category is left out of the automatic charge until you check it. A bank that asks for 3DS authentication holds the charge until the customer opens the link. The 3DS article sends that link by email and SMS, and SMS notifications are on Growing.

A hold is not the charge. BookingKoala's card-hold article places a pre-authorization on the day of the job or a set number of days before it. ZenMaid's payments page says deposits and pre-authorizations are not supported, and the charge article says Stripe and Square do not place a hold through ZenMaid.

The card you already have may not move. ZenMaid cannot charge cards that were saved in Stripe before you connect it. Square can import cards into ZenMaid. BookingKoala's FAQ says cards in a processor such as Stripe can be matched to customer profiles, and the import tool is on Starter. BookingKoala connects one processor at a time. Switching processors is a separate job from turning on the charge.

Invoices are easy to misread as the automatic charge. ZenMaid invoices are manual, and paying one stores the card. BookingKoala invoicing is on every plan, and sending the invoice by itself is a manual link until Premium. Premium is also the plan that lists partial payments, invoice tips, and automatic invoice reminders. A customer can have only one recurring booking-invoice schedule. That limit is on the invoice, not a statement that the customer can have only one recurring clean.

Branding is a separate line from the form. ZenMaid Pro's form carries ZenMaid branding. Your own branding on that form is Pro Max, at $49. BookingKoala Starter includes your own domain. Removing BookingKoala branding is checked on Growing. Hiding the BookingKoala line on an invoice is listed as Premium.

Both sites lead with results and customer quotes. Those are the vendors' claims. Nothing on this page comes from our using either app on a house.

Starter's card does not list it.

**Which plan charges the card after the clean without me opening the job?**

BookingKoala's payments article charges a finished booking immediately, a set number of hours later, or the next day at a time you set. Finished means the scheduled length has passed. A 3-hour job at 8:00 a.m. is charged at 11:01 a.m. if the setting is immediate. That article does not name Starter, Growing, or Premium. The pricing table checks charging and refunding cards on Starter. ZenMaid's processor article says a repeating charge is configured in Stripe or Square, and that ZenMaid does not start it. You can charge a saved card from the appointment or in bulk from unpaid appointments. Marking the appointment paid does not charge it.

**Which cards can I connect, and what is the fee?**

ZenMaid connects a Stripe account or a Square account, directly. The payments page says ZenMaid adds no fee on top of that processor. ZenMaid does not publish the rate. BookingKoala connects one of Stripe, Square, PayPal through Braintree, or Authorize.net, and only one at a time. The processor overview says BookingKoala adds no transaction fee. It does not publish the processor's rate either. Gift cards and referral credits, as a way to pay, are Growing or Premium.

**I have weekly and every-other-week houses. Which plan stores them?**

ZenMaid's calendar color key includes weekly and bi-weekly. A recurring request on the booking form converts to a subscription. Those articles do not name a plan. Scheduling is on Starter, up to 40 appointments that month. The booking form is on the Pro card. BookingKoala's recurring-invoice table names every week, every 2 weeks, every 3 weeks, and every 4 weeks as booking frequencies. **I clean alone. What if I hire four cleaners?**

On BookingKoala, one provider fits on Starter, and five providers are included at $27, if you stay inside 5 GB. Four hired cleaners plus an owner who also cleans is five providers. Texts, the native apps, and GPS are Growing, at $57, which includes 15 providers. On ZenMaid, the owner stays in the base price. The June 4, 2026 cost article charges each other profile $14 on Pro, which is $39 + $56 = $95 for four added profiles. That amount is not printed on the pricing page. Pro is the card with the booking form. Starter's 40-appointment cap still applies on the $19 plan.

**Can I take a deposit, or hold the card, before the first clean?**

ZenMaid's payments page says deposits and pre-authorizations are not supported. The charge article says Stripe and Square do not place a hold through ZenMaid. They check that the card is valid when you charge it. BookingKoala's card-hold article places a pre-authorization on the day of the booking or a set number of days before it, for manual charges, automatic charges, or both. That article does not name a plan. The hold is not the same setting as the charge after the job.
