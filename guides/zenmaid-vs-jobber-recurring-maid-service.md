A recurring maid service is usually buying software for a week that is already booked: the Tuesday house, the every-other-week house, a text so the client expects the team, the checklist the cleaner marks off on the phone, the invoice or the card charge for that visit or for the month, and the hours or the percentage that become the cleaner's pay. This page compares [ZenMaid](https://get.zenmaid.com/pricing) and [Jobber](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/) on that work for a small residential maid service or house-cleaning company. It is a comparison of the vendors' pricing pages and help centers, checked on November 24, 2026. We have not run either product on a house.

A one-person cleaner who is also comparing Square, BookingKoala, Housecall Pro, or FreshBooks should use the [solo house cleaning guide]({{ '/guides/best-software-solo-house-cleaning-business/' | relative_url }}). A company choosing between Jobber and Housecall Pro, including routes and review requests, should use the [residential cleaning comparison]({{ '/guides/jobber-vs-housecall-pro-residential-cleaning/' | relative_url }}). This page stays with ZenMaid and Jobber.

## Who it's for

Use this if you run residential house cleaning or a maid service, most of the book is weekly or every other week, and you are the owner who still builds the week. That includes an owner who cleans alone, an owner plus a few cleaners who each need the day's list, and a small team of up to about ten people who open the schedule.

It is not a commercial janitorial system. It does not cover Housecall Pro, Square, BookingKoala, or FreshBooks except to point at the guides above. ZenMaid's pricing page is written for a maid service. Jobber's plans are the same plans it sells to other home-service trades. The cleaning examples in Jobber's job article are a weekly clean and a move-out clean.

## Quick answer

Prices below are the ones to budget. The pricing section has the date, the billing term, and the page each number came from. Jobber's card also shows a new-customer promotion for subscriptions started before November 30, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. PT. Budget the regular price. The promotion is printed separately in the pricing section.

**One login, and the month stays at or under 40 appointments.** [ZenMaid Starter](https://get.zenmaid.com/pricing) is **$19 per month**. The owner profile is included. Starter includes scheduling, invoicing, and online payments through a Stripe or Square account you connect. It stops at 40 appointments in that month. Booking forms, digital checklists, cleaner GPS, and payroll are on Pro at **$39 per month**. SMS is extra on every plan.

[Jobber Core](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-core-plan/) is the one-person plan: **$29 per month billed annually**, or **$49 month-to-month**. Core includes the calendar, online booking, recurring jobs, quotes, invoices, and card payments. The [Connect card](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/) is where automated client reminders, automatic payments, checklists, time tracking, and QuickBooks Online are named. One Connect user is **$99 per month billed annually**, or **$139 month-to-month**.

Buy Starter if the book is still small, you want the calendar in a cleaning product, and you can live without the Pro list. Buy Core if one login needs recurring jobs and quotes with no visit cap in the plan article, and automatic charging can wait. The solo guide is the page that puts these two next to the other one-person products.

**A recurring book, with cleaners on the app.** The ZenMaid plan that lists the cleaner tools is Pro, at **$39 per month**, plus each added profile. The [cost article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/2423512-how-much-does-zenmaid-cost), dated June 4, 2026, lists that add-on at **$14 per active cleaner, contractor, or office manager** on Pro. The pricing page fetched on November 24, 2026 prints $39 and a team-size control, and it does not print $14. An owner plus four cleaner profiles is $39 + $56 = **$95** if that rate still applies. That is arithmetic from the cost article, not a bundle the pricing page prints. Pro lists unlimited appointments, digital checklists, the mobile app with cleaner GPS, booking forms with ZenMaid branding, more message templates, and payroll. The QuickBooks line is marked "COMING SOON." ZenMaid's processor article says a repeating card charge is configured in Stripe or Square. ZenMaid does not start it. Invoices are created by hand.

Jobber Connect for five users is **$149 per month billed annually**, **$169 per month on a 1-year term billed monthly**, or **$199 month-to-month**. The Connect article names automatic payments and says the plan supports up to five users. The Connect card lists the reminders, the checklists, the time tracking, and QuickBooks Online. Jobber's confirm-payroll article says Jobber does not pay employees. Approved hours can sync to Gusto on Connect or higher, in the United States, and only for hourly employees in Gusto. A contractor or a commission pay type is outside that sync.

Buy Pro if the week is a cleaning route, the cleaners need a checklist and a clock, and payroll has to understand an hourly rate or a percentage of the visit, on a bill you can cancel at the end of the month. Buy Connect if the saved card has to be charged when the recurring invoice is created, or QuickBooks Online has to sync now. At five people, Connect's regular annual price is $149. Pro's total for five profiles is the $39 base plus four times the per-profile rate, which the cost article prints and the pricing page does not.

**About ten logins.** The Grow article says the plan supports up to 10 users. The 10-user Grow pack is **$299 billed annually**, **$349 on the 1-year monthly term**, or **$399 month-to-month**. The pricing page also lists a 10-user Connect pack at **$229 billed annually**. The Connect article says Connect supports up to five users. See unverified claims. On the cost article's Pro rate, nine added profiles are $126, and $39 + $126 = **$165**. Pro Max is **$49** plus **$24** per added profile in that same article, which is $49 + $216 = **$265** for nine added profiles. Those ZenMaid totals are the same kind of arithmetic.

## Choose ZenMaid if

Choose ZenMaid if the product you are buying is a maid-service calendar, and the people on the phone are cleaners.

Pro is the card that lists digital checklists, payroll, cleaner GPS, and booking forms. A weekly or every-other-week house is a recurring service. The calendar color key names weekly, bi-weekly, every three weeks, and monthly. A house cleaned on two different weekdays is two weekly series, one per day. The same recurring service can carry the assigned cleaners and the checklist forward.

Choose ZenMaid if cleaner pay is a percentage of the visit, a flat amount, or an hourly rate from the clock, and you want that math in the cleaning account. The payroll article covers those pay types, then an export or an email of the approved summary. Choose it if you will stay month to month. The pricing FAQ says there is no contract.

Choose ZenMaid if you will send invoices by hand, or charge the saved card yourself from the appointment, and a repeating charge can live in Stripe or Square. Pro Max, at $49 plus the higher per-profile rate, is the card that lists your own branding on the booking form, every message template, service ratings, and cleaner availability with paid time off.

## Choose Jobber if

Choose Jobber if the repeating house has to invoice and charge a saved card inside the same product. That feature is named on Connect. Core already stores the weekly, biweekly, or monthly series, and the customer can approve a quote and pay online. Connect is the step up for the automatic charge, the appointment reminder, the checklist, and QuickBooks Online.

Choose Jobber if the cleaners are hourly employees and the hours should land in Gusto. The Gusto article says Connect or higher, United States only, and hourly employee types only. Choose Jobber if the quote has to offer the standard clean and the add-ons, the fridge and the inside of the oven, and the customer picks. Those optional line items are on Grow. A company that assigns each cleaner by name can do that on Connect or Grow. Crew scheduling is on the Plus card, and Plus starts at five users.

Choose Jobber if you will stay the year for the annual price. The annual price and the 1-year monthly price are a 12-month term. Core is the lower bill when one person only needs the calendar, the quote, and the invoice, and the Connect list can wait.

## ZenMaid

ZenMaid's [pricing page](https://get.zenmaid.com/pricing) sells three month-to-month plans to a maid service. Starter is $19, Pro is $39, and Pro Max is $49. Each card says SMS charges are not included. The [cost article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/2423512-how-much-does-zenmaid-cost), dated June 4, 2026, says only the owner profile is included and every other profile is an extra charge. A cleaner who needs the app is that extra profile. The same article says annual plans exist on request and does not print an annual price.

**Scheduling.** Starter includes scheduling with calendar, dispatch, and map views, and it stops at 40 appointments in the month. The pricing FAQ says that at the cap you cannot add another appointment that month, and the account sends you to upgrade. Pro lists unlimited appointments. Ten weekly houses are 40 visits in a four-week month and 50 in a five-week month. That is arithmetic from the cap, not a package ZenMaid sells. A route that crosses 40 visits in a month is Pro.

The [color-code article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/3620974-how-to-color-code-the-appointments-on-the-calendar), dated April 22, 2026, is the list of frequencies the calendar already knows: weekly, bi-weekly, every three weeks, monthly or every four weeks, and one-time. The [daily-service article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/1768662-how-to-add-a-daily-recurring-service-in-zenmaid), dated December 5, 2025, says a house cleaned on more than one weekday is a separate weekly recurring appointment for each day, so Monday and Thursday can be paused independently. The [frequency article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/370961-how-to-edit-the-frequency-of-a-recurring-service), dated February 10, 2026, changes the repeat, the day, and the start and end time from a chosen future appointment. The [recurring-service page](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/14112225-how-to-use-the-manage-recurring-service-page-mrsp), dated April 29, 2026, holds the price, the assigned cleaners or team, and the checklist, and an edit can apply to future appointments. None of those articles name Starter, Pro, or Pro Max. Spotfinder, "easy scheduling of new recurring customers," is on the Pro card.

**Notifications.** The Starter card says "Limited automated SMS & Emails." Pro says "More." Pro Max says "All." The [SMS article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/370973-sms-templates-in-zenmaid), dated October 23, 2026, is the list this guide uses for texts. On all plans it includes the work order, clock-in and clock-out reminders, key pickup and drop-off reminders, the SOS alert, appointment confirmation, the appointment reminder, a second appointment reminder, the invoice notification, and a tip request. Pro and Pro Max add the cleaner cancellation notice and the customer cancellation notice. Pro Max only adds cleaning complete, the appointment follow-up, the service rating, the service-rating follow-up that asks for a Google review, and the on-my-way text. New templates cannot be created. Existing wording can be edited. Texts are limited to 156 characters, they are sent from a do-not-reply number, and the article says that sender is included at no extra cost. The message bundle itself is extra. See the pricing section.

The [getting-started guide](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/8757355-get-started-with-zenmaid-the-basics), dated July 6, 2026, names a stricter split. It puts clock-in and clock-out reminders and appointment confirmation on Pro and Pro Max, and it keeps the customer appointment reminder on Starter. Both pages agree that the customer appointment reminder is on Starter, and that the on-my-way text and the cleaning-complete text are Pro Max. Where they disagree, see unverified claims.

The cleaner app article describes an On my way action and a Notify job complete action on the appointment. It does not name a plan. The SMS list marks OMW Text and Cleaning Complete as Pro Max, and OMW Text is not in the column of templates sent from the appointment's manual text action.

**Checklists.** The Pro card lists digital checklists. The [checklist article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/10496545-digital-cleaning-checklist), dated October 1, 2026, does not name a plan. One checklist can be assigned to an appointment. The assignment is that appointment only, or that appointment and all future appointments in the recurring service. Sections are rooms or areas. When a cleaner marks an item, the account stores the cleaner and the time. A share link is read-only. The client sees progress, the date and time, and the assigned cleaner. The client does not see who completed each item or the internal comments. The link can be placed in an email or text template.

**Online booking and quotes.** The Pro card lists booking forms with ZenMaid branding. Pro Max lists booking forms with your own branding. Starter's card does not list them. The [time-slot article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/3518448-how-to-create-booking-form-time-slots-and-edit-bookable-days), dated July 3, 2026, says the form is not a live calendar. The suggested wording is that you will confirm the slot the customer picked. The form can offer a recurrence. The [pricing article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/3242732-how-to-price-your-booking-form), dated September 10, 2026, says bedroom, bathroom, square-footage, and hour sliders scale by a fixed step, extras are flat fees, and a recurrence option can carry a discount. The [quote article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/6278498-how-to-create-and-customize-an-estimate-or-quote-form-in-zenmaid) is that booking form renamed as an estimate, with prices shown or hidden. It does not name a plan. The SMS article says the booking form is included in the paid plan. Starter is a paid plan, and Starter's card does not list the form. See unverified claims.

**Invoicing and payments.** Starter includes invoicing and online payments with Stripe and Square. The [charge article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/12099721-ways-customers-can-be-charged-by-card-in-zenmaid), dated January 30, 2026, says a card has to be saved on the customer before ZenMaid can charge it, and that marking an appointment paid does not charge the card. You can charge one appointment, bulk-charge unpaid appointments, or charge from the contact. For a weekly or monthly clean, the same article says you charge each appointment, charge the unpaid invoices on one day, or put the month's total on one appointment in the series. Invoices are created by hand. When the customer pays the invoice, ZenMaid marks the linked appointment paid.

The [processor article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/4801218-connecting-a-credit-card-processor-to-zenmaid), dated January 29, 2026, says the account has to be Stripe or Square directly. Recurring or automatic charges are configured in Stripe or Square. ZenMaid does not start them. Cards already stored in Stripe before the connection cannot be charged through ZenMaid. Square has an import for cards already saved there. ZenMaid does not publish the card rate. The rate is the processor's.

**Payroll and the phone.** The Pro card lists payroll. The [payroll article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/1767506-how-to-run-payroll-in-zenmaid), dated March 17, 2026, does not name a plan. A cleaner can be hourly, revenue share, a fixed flat rate, or a variable flat rate. For hourly pay, clocked time is used when the cleaner clocks in and out. Otherwise the scheduled appointment time is used. Revenue share is calculated on the original appointment price, before discounts, taxes, overcharges, and tips. The article says a revenue-share cleaner can see that job cost. Tips are listed and are not added until someone enters them as an extra. One payroll run cannot mix hourly and revenue share for the same cleaner. The article's path is to run one period, change the rate, and run the other period. After approval, the summary can be printed, exported as a CSV, or emailed to the cleaners. The article describes that summary. It does not describe a direct deposit or a payroll-tax filing. Cleaners can be allowed to open their own approved payroll on the web app. Payroll notes are visible to them when that access is on.

The [cleaner app article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/6968490-zenmaid-mobile-app-overview-for-cleaners), dated September 28, 2026, says the app is for cleaners and office staff. Customers cannot sign in. The day is listed from earliest to latest. The appointment shows the frequency, the address, clock in and clock out, the checklist, notes, and an SOS alert. The Starter card lists the SOS alert. The daily route map numbers the stops in schedule order. The GPS section says the location is recorded when Clock In or Clock Out is pressed, that tracking is not continuous, and that it does not run outside the app. The Pro card is the line that adds cleaner GPS. The [clock article](https://zenmaid.com/answers/en/articles/1768986-how-cleaners-clock-in-and-out-using-the-zenmaid-mobile-app) says an app clock-in stores a timestamp that cannot be edited, a clock-in entered on the website can be edited, and a cleaner clocks in and out of an appointment once.

Pro Max lists cleaner availability and paid-time-off tracking, service ratings, data export, Mailchimp and Zapier, your branding on the booking form, and every communication template. The QuickBooks line on the Pro card says "COMING SOON."

The trial is 14 days. The pricing FAQ says you can cancel the trial or the subscription from the account settings, and that the plan is month-to-month with no contract. The cost article says a cancelled plan stays active through the end of the billing period. The page does not say whether the trial asks for a card.

## Jobber

Jobber's plan articles and the [pricing table](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/) name Core, Connect, Grow, and Plus. On that page, Grow is "All Connect features, plus" the Grow list, and Plus is "All Grow features, plus" the Plus list. A tool named on Connect is on Grow and Plus as well. [Core](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-core-plan/), updated November 18, 2026, is one user and an unlimited number of quotes, jobs, and invoices. [Connect](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-connect-plan/), updated the same day, is where automatic payments, routing, automated reminders, and QuickBooks Online are named. The Connect article says the plan supports up to five users. [Grow](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/the-grow-plan/), updated the same day, adds the quote choices, two-way texting, Find a Time, location timers, and job costing on one-off jobs, and it says the plan supports up to 10 users.

**Scheduling.** Core includes requests, online booking, and the calendar. A job is one-off or recurring. [Job basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/job-basics/), updated November 23, 2026, uses weekly cleaning as the recurring example and a move-out clean as the one-off example. A one-off job can hold up to 20 visits and still is not a repeating schedule. The same article says the job type cannot be switched later. A one-off deep clean cannot be turned into the weekly clean. Recreate it.

[Recurring jobs](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/create-a-recurring-job/), updated November 12, 2026, repeat weekly, biweekly, monthly, or on a custom pattern, including two days a week or the first and third Friday. Billing is per visit, or a fixed price for the period, such as one amount per month. Jobber's own guidance is to end recurring jobs within a few years, because a very long schedule creates enough visits to slow the account down. A job close to its end can be extended by editing the end date. Visit-specific line items, the one-time oven on a regular clean, transfer to a per-visit invoice. On a fixed-price job they do not transfer. Checklists attached on the job are added to every visit.

The Connect article says the plan can route the day's work and assign an incoming job to the closest person on the map. It also lists live vehicle GPS through FleetSharp, including speed, direction, fuel level, mileage, and a check-engine light. The article does not say the hardware is included in the subscription. Find a Time is named in the Grow article, which describes it as highlighting open availability in the calendar. The pricing page describes Find a Time as recommendations based on team availability and travel times.

**Notifications and the client.** The Connect card adds "Send automated client reminders" on top of Core. The Connect article describes automated emails and texts, including appointment reminders. The Core article says on-my-way texts are not on Core. Job basics says a booking-confirmation email is on select plans and does not name the plan. Two-way texting is on Grow, in the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom. Messages from the team go out on one dedicated number.

Core's article includes client hub for requests, quotes the customer can approve online, and invoices the customer can pay online. The pricing page describes client hub as the place a client approves a quote, checks appointment details, and pays.

**Quotes.** Core quotes use your branding. There is no cap on how many you send, and the customer approves them online. The Core article says optional line items are not on Core. Grow adds them: add-ons or packages, images, and a markup that shows an estimated margin. The pricing page says reviews inside that advanced quote layout require the Reviews add-on.

**Invoicing and payments.** On Core, an invoice is created from the job and the customer can pay it online. Connect adds automatic reminders for unpaid invoices and for quotes that have not been approved. On Grow, the Connect follow-ups stay on a preset schedule, and the custom automation builder is the tool for your own timing.

Jobber Payments is on Core in the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom. The pricing table shows the same three rates on Core, Connect, Grow, and Plus: cards at 2.9% + 30¢, Tap to Pay on the phone at 2.7% + 30¢, and US bank payments (ACH) at 1%. The table shows one card rate. It does not publish a separate rate for American Express or for a card kept on file. The Core article says instant payouts to a debit card are available for an extra 1%.

[Automatic payments](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/automatic-payments/) charge a card or ACH account saved through Jobber Payments when a recurring job's invoice is created. The Connect article names that feature. The pricing page's line says the charge uses card processing rates, and that the feature is available in the United States and Canada. The help article says select plans. The charge follows the job's billing schedule: after each completed visit, on the last day of the month, on a custom schedule, or when the job is closed. "As needed" does not work with automatic payments. A failed charge is attempted once. The invoice stays open, and an admin is notified. A monthly invoice is created at 9 p.m. in the company's timezone on the scheduled day, so a clean done that day is still included. The customer gets a receipt email when the charge succeeds.

**Checklists, time, and payroll.** The Connect card lists "Standardize job documentation with checklists" and "Track time and expenses." The [checklist article](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/checklists/), updated November 17, 2026, says checklists are on select plans. ## What to watch out for

Count the limit the plan actually uses. ZenMaid Starter counts appointments in the month, and it stops at 40. A weekly house is one appointment each time it is on the calendar, and a five-week month is more appointments than a four-week month. Jobber Core's article says quotes, jobs, and invoices are unlimited, and the limit there is one user. A recurring maid route that is already past a handful of weekly houses is past Starter even when the owner is the only cleaner.

A Jobber user is someone who opens the schedule. The pricing page says that directly. The ZenMaid owner is included. A cleaner profile, a contractor profile, or an office manager on ZenMaid is the extra charge in the cost article, and only an active role is charged. The same article says the people on the account have to match the workforce. A cleaner who needs the day's list on a phone is a ZenMaid profile and a Jobber user. Core is one user in the plan article. Five people with five phones is a five-user Jobber plan, or the ZenMaid base plus four profiles.

Build the repeat clean in the shape the product stores. In ZenMaid, a two-day-a-week house is two weekly series, and the series can be edited from a future visit. In Jobber, create a recurring job at the start. A one-off deep clean will not convert later. End the series within a few years, then extend it, because Jobber says a very long schedule slows the account down. A one-time oven on a per-visit invoice can be a line on that visit. On a fixed monthly price, that line does not transfer.

Count the card charge you will actually run. ZenMaid does not publish a rate and does not start the repeating charge. Stripe or Square does, and invoices in ZenMaid are created by hand. Marking the visit paid does not charge the card. Jobber names the automatic charge on Connect, for a recurring job, on the billing schedule you set. "As needed" billing does not take that charge. A failed charge is attempted once. The pricing page points at the published card rate, 2.9% + 30¢ for a card the customer pays online, and does not list a second rate for a card kept on file. The help article also allows a saved bank account, at the published 1% ACH rate for a US bank payment. Tap to Pay in Jobber is 2.7% + 30¢. ZenMaid's rate for the same tap is whatever Stripe or Square charges on the account you connect.

Payroll is a different object in each product. ZenMaid Pro's article calculates hourly pay, a percentage of the original visit price, or a flat rate, and then exports or emails the summary. A percentage cleaner can see the visit price. Jobber's article says Jobber does not pay the employee. Gusto receives approved hours for hourly US employees on Connect or higher, and it does not receive contractor, salary, or commission types. A maid service that pays cleaners a percentage, or pays them as contractors, is looking at ZenMaid's payroll article or at a payroll tool outside Jobber. Location timers, the automatic clock from the phone's position, are on Grow. ZenMaid's GPS record is the clock-in and clock-out point, on the Pro card, and the app article says it is not a live track.

The message plan is easy to misread. ZenMaid's cards say limited, more, and all. The October 23, 2026 SMS article and the July 6, 2026 getting-started list do not draw that line in the same place. The customer reminder is on Starter in both. The on-my-way text is Pro Max in both. Confirm the others in the account before you buy Starter to get confirmation texts. SMS bundles are extra on every ZenMaid plan. Jobber's reminder line is Connect. On-my-way texts are excluded from Core, and the plan that adds them is not named. Two-way texting is Grow.

Jobber's $29 and $149 prices, for one Core user and for five Connect users, are a one-year term when they are the annual figures. The no-commitment prices are $49 and $199. The lower introductory figures last three months, or 12 months on the annual column, for new customers who subscribe before November 30, 2026. ZenMaid's $19 and $39 are the month-to-month prices the pricing page prints.

There is no cleaning package that moves Jobber's Grow or Plus tools onto Core. Optional quote choices, two-way texting, and location timers stay on Grow. Crew scheduling and the included Marketing Suite stay on Plus. ZenMaid's QuickBooks line is still marked coming soon. Jobber's pricing FAQ answers the accounting question with QuickBooks Online.

Both sites lead with results and customer quotes. Those are the vendors' claims. Nothing on this page comes from our using either app on a house.

## FAQ

**I clean houses every week and every other week. Which plan puts them on a calendar?**

ZenMaid Starter, until the month hits 40 appointments. The calendar color key includes weekly and bi-weekly, and the recurring-service articles do not name a plan. Pro is the plan with unlimited appointments and Spotfinder. Jobber Core, as a recurring job. Weekly, biweekly, monthly, and custom patterns are in the recurring-job article, and the Core workflow includes recurring jobs. One user is $29 per month billed annually, or $49 with no commitment. Automatic charging of that series is named on Connect.

**Five of us need the app. What bill should we expect?**

On ZenMaid Pro, the owner is in the $39 base and the June 4, 2026 cost article charges each of the other four profiles $14, which is $95. That amount is not printed on the pricing page. Pro is the card with checklists, payroll, GPS, and booking forms. On Jobber, use a five-user plan. Connect is $199 month-to-month or $149 billed annually, with a one-year term on the annual price. That plan's article names automatic payments, and the card names reminders, checklists, time tracking, and QuickBooks Online. Grow is $299 month-to-month or $229 billed annually if the quote needs optional add-ons or you want location timers. A new-customer Jobber promotion lowers the first months for subscriptions started before November 30, 2026. Budget the regular price.

**Can the client pay a card without a phone call after each clean?**

Jobber names that on Connect, for recurring jobs only, using a card or bank account saved through Jobber Payments. The charge follows the billing schedule, including after each completed visit or on the last day of the month at 9 p.m. in the company's timezone. A failed charge is attempted once. The pricing page points at the published card rates and does not list a separate saved-card rate. ZenMaid's help center says a repeating charge is set up in Stripe or Square, and that ZenMaid invoices are created by hand. You can also charge a saved card from the appointment or in bulk. Marking the appointment paid does not charge it. ZenMaid does not publish the card rate.

**Do the cleaners get a checklist and a text before the visit?**

ZenMaid lists digital checklists on Pro. One checklist can follow every future visit in the recurring service. The October 23, 2026 SMS article includes an appointment reminder on all plans, and it puts the on-my-way text on Pro Max. The July 6, 2026 getting-started list also puts the appointment reminder on Starter and the on-my-way text on Pro Max, and it disagrees on other templates. Jobber lists checklists and automated client reminders on the Connect card. A checklist attached to the recurring job is on every visit. The Core article says on-my-way texts are not on Core.

**We pay cleaners a percentage of the clean. Which plan calculates that?**

ZenMaid's payroll article covers revenue share, hourly, and flat rate. Revenue share uses the original appointment price, and the article says the cleaner can see that price. Payroll is on the Pro card. The article covers the calculation, the approval, and an export or an email. It does not describe a direct deposit. Jobber's confirm-payroll article says Jobber does not pay employees. Gusto syncs approved hours for hourly employees, on Connect or higher, in the United States. Contractor and commission types are not synced. A percentage paid to a contractor is outside that integration.

**The customer wants to book the next clean online. What is on the entry plan?**

Jobber Core includes online booking and request forms, and the pricing page says the booking is scheduled from your preferences. The Connect article adds booking from Google through Local Services Ads. ZenMaid lists booking forms on Pro, at $39, with ZenMaid branding. Pro Max, at $49, is the card that lists your own branding on the form. The time-slot article says the form is not a live calendar and that you confirm the slot. The SMS article calls the booking form part of the paid plan and does not name Pro. Starter's card does not list the form.
