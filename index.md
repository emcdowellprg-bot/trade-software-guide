This site publishes plain comparisons of the software a small service business actually has to pick: scheduling, quotes, invoices, payments, and the maintenance work that comes back next season.

The businesses in mind are one to a few trucks. HVAC, plumbing, lawn care, cleaning, dog grooming, pressure washing, and the trades next to them. The person reading is usually the owner, not a software team.

A guide answers one buying question. It says who each product fits, what the feature differences change in a normal week, and what the vendor charges, with the date that price was checked and a link to the page it came from. There are no affiliate links on the site yet. Links go to the vendor's own site. We do not invent reviews, and we do not claim to have run a product on a job unless that is true. This first guide is a document comparison, not a field test.

How a guide is put together, and the affiliate disclosure, is on the [About]({{ '/about/' | relative_url }}) page.
